## Export: what is in it

An owner or accountant can request an export. Muhasib.ai prepares a ZIP with **one CSV for every table of this company only** (invoices, bills, journals, contacts, items, payroll and more), your uploaded documents and receipts, and a **manifest** listing row counts and a checksum for each file. Other companies' data and secrets such as passwords and API keys are never included. When it is ready, download it from the same page; the link works for 24 hours.

## Delete the company

Only a company **owner** can delete the company. Enter your password, your authenticator code if you use two-step verification, and type the company name.

- The company is hidden immediately.
- You have **30 days to restore** it from the banner on the page, or from the sign-in screen if you have no other company.
- After 30 days, personal data (contacts, employee details, member access) is anonymised or removed.
- API keys are revoked at once and are not restored.

## What we keep

Accounting records (journals, invoices, bills, VAT filings and their evidence) stay read-only for **five years**, as UAE law requires, and are then erased. They are not used for anything else, and cannot be deleted before then.

## Companies managed by an accounting firm

The firm owner must confirm the deletion before the 30 days begin.

> Export your data first. After the 30 days the company cannot be restored.

Open [Data and privacy](/settings/data).

## Export

An owner or accountant can request an export. Muhasib.ai prepares a ZIP file with every table of the company as CSV, your uploaded documents, and a manifest with a checksum for each file. When it is ready, download it from the same page. The link works for 24 hours. Request a new export if it expires.

## Delete the company

Only a company **owner** can delete the company. To confirm it is you, enter your password, your authenticator code if you use two-step verification, and type the company name.

- The company is hidden immediately.
- You have **30 days to restore** it from the banner on the page, or from the sign-in screen if you have no other company.
- After 30 days, personal data is anonymised and access is removed.
- API keys are revoked at once and are not restored.

## What we keep

Accounting records (journals, invoices, bills, VAT filings and their evidence) stay read-only for five years, as UAE law requires, and are then erased. They are never used for anything else.

## Companies managed by an accounting firm

The firm owner must confirm the deletion before the 30 days begin.

> Export your data first. After the 30 days it cannot be restored.

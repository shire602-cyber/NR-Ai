A quote shows a customer what you will charge. It posts nothing to your books until it becomes an invoice.

## Create and send

1. Open [Quotes](/quotes) and choose **New quote**.
2. Choose the customer, add lines and set an expiry date.
3. Download the PDF or email it.

## When the customer accepts

Mark the quote as accepted, then convert it to an invoice. Lines, prices and VAT carry across, so nothing is retyped.

## Statuses

A quote moves from draft to sent, then accepted, rejected or expired, and finally converted once it becomes an invoice. Only an accepted quote should be converted.

## Good to know

- Quotes and invoices have separate number sequences.
- A quote can be converted once. Edit the invoice afterwards if the customer changes the order.

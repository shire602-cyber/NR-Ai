[Invoice templates](/invoice-templates) controls the layout of the PDF your customers receive.

## Customise

- Upload your logo.
- Pick the accent colour and the layout.
- Choose English, Arabic or a bilingual invoice.
- Add payment details and a footer note, such as your bank account and terms.

## Preview

The preview updates as you change settings, so you see the real invoice before saving.

## Good to know

- Templates change how an invoice looks, never its numbers or VAT.
- Every tax invoice still shows your name, TRN, the customer details and the VAT amount, as UAE rules require.
- Invoice numbering and default terms live in your company settings. See [Company settings](/help/company-settings).

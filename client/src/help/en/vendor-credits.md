When a supplier credits you for returned or overcharged goods, record it in [Vendor credits](/vendor-credits).

## Create a vendor credit

1. Choose **New vendor credit** and pick the vendor.
2. Optionally link it to the bill it corrects.
3. Enter the lines and VAT.

## Approve

Approving posts the credit. It reverses the original expense and the input VAT, and appears on your VAT return. Reverse-charge treatment follows the related bill.

## Apply

Apply the credit to open bills of the same vendor. The bill balance falls by the amount applied.

## Good to know

- An approved credit cannot be edited. Void it to reverse its entry, then enter a corrected one.
- Your own credit notes to customers are in [Credit notes](/help/credit-notes).

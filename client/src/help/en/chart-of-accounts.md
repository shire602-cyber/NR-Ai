The chart of accounts groups your money into assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses. Every invoice, bill and journal entry is posted to these accounts.

## View and edit

Open [Chart of accounts](/chart-of-accounts). Each account has a code, an English and an Arabic name and a type. You can add accounts, rename them and archive the ones you no longer use.

## Add accounts in bulk

Use [Import data](/import) and choose **Chart of accounts** to bring accounts from Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Xero or a spreadsheet. Accounts already in your chart are skipped by code.

## Open an account's ledger

Click an account to see every posting, with a running balance. Click a line to open the document behind it.

## Good to know

- An account that has postings cannot be deleted. Archive it instead.
- System accounts, such as accounts receivable, VAT payable and opening balance equity, are used automatically and are protected from deletion.
- Choose the account type carefully: it decides where the account appears on the balance sheet and profit and loss.

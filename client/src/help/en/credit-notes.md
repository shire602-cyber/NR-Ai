A credit note reduces what a customer owes. It reverses the revenue and the output VAT of the invoice it corrects.

## Issue a credit note

1. Open [Credit notes](/credit-notes) and choose **New credit note**, or start from the invoice itself.
2. Pick the invoice, and the lines and amounts to credit. You can credit part of an invoice.
3. Issue it. The credit appears on the VAT return of the period it is dated in.

## Use the credit

- **Apply it** to the customer's other open invoices.
- **Refund it** by recording a refund to the customer's bank account.
- Leave it on account for the next invoice.

## Good to know

- A credit note can never exceed the invoice amount still open.
- Supplier credits are recorded on the [Vendor credits](/help/vendor-credits) page.

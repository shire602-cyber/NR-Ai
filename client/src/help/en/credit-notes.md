A credit note reduces what a customer owes. It reverses the revenue and the output VAT of the invoice line it corrects.

## One way to create a credit note

Open the invoice and choose **Create credit note**. There is no separate "New credit note" button: a credit note always belongs to an invoice. The [Credit notes](/credit-notes) page lists the ones you have issued.

1. Tick the **lines** to credit and set the quantity or amount for each. You can credit part of an invoice, line by line.
2. Choose the **credit note date**. It defaults to today. The VAT correction lands in the period of this date, so pick the date the correction belongs to. A locked period or a VAT period that has been filed cannot be chosen.
3. Issue it.

## Use the credit

- **Apply it** to the customer's other open invoices.
- **Refund it** by recording a refund to the customer's bank account.
- Leave it on account for the next invoice.

## Good to know

- A credit note can never exceed what is still creditable on the invoice.
- The invoice status follows its payments and credits. See [Create and send VAT invoices](/help/invoices).
- Supplier credits are recorded on the [Vendor credits](/help/vendor-credits) page.

[Approvals](/approvals) lists documents waiting for a signature, and holds the rules that decide who must approve what.

## Rules

A rule applies when a document's amount in dirhams is above its threshold. The highest matching threshold wins. A document with no matching rule is approved in one step. Only the company owner can change rules.

## Documents that can need approval

Bills, expense claims, purchase orders, payroll runs and manual journal entries.

## Approve

Open a waiting document and approve it. When every required person has signed, it is approved and posts. You cannot approve a document you prepared yourself when a rule requires a second person.

## Reject and resubmit

**Rejecting a document ends the request.** The document leaves the approval queue and cannot be approved or posted. Give a reason: it goes back to the person who prepared it. They fix it and **resubmit**, which starts a new request that every approver signs again.

## When you are the only approver

If a rule names a single approver and that person also prepared the document, the system asks you to **acknowledge** that you are approving your own document before it goes through. The acknowledgement is recorded.

## Good to know

- Every decision is recorded with the name and time.

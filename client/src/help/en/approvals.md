[Approvals](/approvals) lists documents waiting for a signature, and holds the rules that decide who must approve what.

## Rules

A rule applies when a document's amount in dirhams is above its threshold. The highest matching threshold wins. A document with no matching rule is approved in one step, as before. Only the company owner can change rules.

## Documents that can need approval

Bills, expense claims, purchase orders, payroll runs and manual journal entries.

## Approve or reject

Open a waiting document and approve it. When every required person has signed, it is approved and posts. If you reject it, say why: the document goes back to the person who prepared it so they can fix it.

## Good to know

- You cannot approve a document you prepared yourself when a rule requires a second person.
- Every decision is recorded with the name and time.

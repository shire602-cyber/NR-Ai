When a customer pays before the work or goods, record it in [Customer advances](/customer-advances).

## VAT on an advance

VAT is due in the period you issue the advance tax invoice, which is when you receive the money. A refundable deposit is outside the scope of VAT.

## Record an advance

1. Choose **New advance** and pick the customer.
2. Enter the amount. Available amounts are shown net of VAT.
3. This issues an advance tax invoice. It is created unpaid: record the payment on it when the money arrives, or mark it received if the money is already in your account.

## Use it

Deduct the advance from the final invoice, so the customer pays only the balance. Or refund it: the refund issues a credit note on the advance invoice and pays the money back. A refund is entered with VAT included.

## Good to know

- Unapplied advances show as a balance for the customer, not as income.
- Your VAT return picks up the advance in the period it was issued. See [VAT returns](/help/vat-filing).

When a customer pays before the work or goods, record it in [Customer advances](/customer-advances).

## Dirhams only

Advances are recorded **in AED only**. If a customer pays in another currency, record the dirham amount you actually received.

## VAT at receipt

VAT is due in the period you receive the money, when you issue the advance tax invoice. A refundable deposit is outside the scope of VAT.

## Record an advance

1. Choose **New advance** and pick the customer.
2. Enter the amount. Available amounts are shown net of VAT.
3. This issues an advance tax invoice. Record the payment on it when the money arrives, or mark it received if the money is already in your account.

## Use it

Deduct the advance from the final invoice, so the customer pays only the balance. Or refund it: the refund issues a credit note on the advance invoice and pays the money back. A refund is entered with VAT included.

## Good to know

- Unapplied advances show as a balance for the customer, not as income.
- Your VAT return picks up the advance in the period it was received. See [VAT returns](/help/vat-filing).

[Payment chasing](/payment-chasing) lists the invoices that are past due and the next reminder each one is eligible for.

## How it works

Reminders escalate in levels, from a friendly nudge to a final notice. Each level has its own wording that you can edit, using placeholders such as the customer name, invoice number and amount due.

## Send a reminder

1. Open the page and review the overdue invoices.
2. Pick the invoices, check the preview of each message and send by email, or copy the text into WhatsApp or your own workflow.
3. Each reminder you send is recorded against the invoice.

## Good to know

- Emails are sent only when an email provider is configured for your account. Otherwise the text is shown for you to copy.
- Amounts in other currencies are shown in dirhams in the totals. Open the table for the amount in the invoice currency.
- Companies scheduled for deletion are skipped.

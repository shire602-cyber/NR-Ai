[Payroll](/payroll) manages employees, monthly pay runs and the files UAE banks expect.

## Set up employees

Add each employee with salary components, joining date and bank details. For WPS you also need each employee's **labour card number (MoL ID), routing code of their bank and IBAN or account number**. The SIF file cannot be created without them.

## Run payroll

1. Create a pay run for the month.
2. Review each employee's pay.
3. Approve the run. It posts salary expense and the amount payable.

**Joiners are pro-rated.** An employee who joins mid-month is paid for the days from the joining date to month end.

**Leave and sick leave.** Unpaid leave and sick-leave deductions are calculated on the **full monthly wage**, not on the pro-rated amount. Paid leave deducts nothing. **Unpaid leave is not service:** unpaid days are left out of the service period for gratuity and earn no annual leave (Federal Decree-Law 33/2021).

## Loans and advances

Record a loan to an employee and the instalments come off pay automatically. A deduction is capped at **20% of the monthly wage**; the rest carries to the next month.

## Final settlement

When someone leaves, the **final settlement** pays the last salary, unused leave and end-of-service gratuity. The gratuity is paid out of the provision already accrued monthly, so the expense is not booked twice; only any shortfall is expensed.

## Pay through WPS

Download the **SIF** file for the run and upload it to your bank, then record the payment when the bank has paid.

## Payslips

Generate payslips as PDFs for every employee.

## Good to know

- Approval rules can require a second person for payroll runs. See [Approvals](/help/approvals).
- Check bank details before downloading the file.

[Payroll](/payroll) manages employees, monthly pay runs and the files UAE banks expect.

## Set up employees

Add each employee with their salary components, bank details and joining date.

## Run payroll

1. Create a pay run for the month.
2. Review each employee's pay and adjust for deductions or extras.
3. Approve the run. It posts salary expense and the amount payable.

## Pay through WPS

Download the **SIF** file for the run and upload it to your bank. After the bank pays, record the payment.

## Payslips

Generate payslips as PDFs for every employee.

## End-of-service gratuity

Gratuity is accrued monthly against a provision, so the liability is on your balance sheet before anyone leaves.

## Good to know

- Approval rules can require a second person to approve payroll runs. See [Approvals](/help/approvals).
- Make sure employee bank details are correct before downloading the file.

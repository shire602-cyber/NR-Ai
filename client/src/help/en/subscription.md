[Subscription](/subscription) shows the plan your company is on.

## Your plan

The page lists what is included, such as the number of users and invoices per month, and how much you have used.

## Upgrade or change

Choose another plan to unlock more. Features that need a higher plan show an **upgrade** prompt that names the plan you need.

## Good to know

- API keys and webhooks are available on the Professional plan and above.
- Your existing records are never deleted because of a plan limit.

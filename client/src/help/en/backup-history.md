## Backups

[Backup and restore](/backup-restore) creates a complete copy of your accounts, invoices, journal entries and receipts. Create one before a big change, and review what a restore would change before you confirm it.

## Activity history

[History](/history) is a log of changes to your financial records: who created, updated or deleted what, and when. Filter by action, record type or text.

## Good to know

- A backup is a safety net, not an archive. To take your data with you, use an export. See [Export and delete your data](/help/data-export-deletion).
- The activity history is read-only and cannot be edited.

[Contacts](/contacts) holds your customers and your vendors. Invoices, quotes, bills and payments all point to a contact.

## Add a contact

Choose **New contact** and enter the name, email, phone, address and payment terms. Add the **TRN** (15 digits) of any VAT-registered business so it prints on tax invoices.

## Customers, vendors or both

Each contact is marked as a customer, a vendor or both, so a company you buy from and sell to appears once.

## Statements

Open a contact to see its invoices, payments and balance. Send a customer a statement of account with one click.

## Import

Bring many contacts at once from a spreadsheet or another system with [Import data](/help/import-data). Duplicates are recognised by tax number, or by name and email.

## Good to know

- Changing a contact's details does not change documents already issued.
- A contact with documents cannot be deleted. Mark it inactive instead.

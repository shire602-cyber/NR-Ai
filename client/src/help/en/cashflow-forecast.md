[Cash flow forecast](/cashflow-forecast) projects your cash from what is already in your books.

## What it uses

- Open invoices by due date, as money coming in.
- Open bills by due date, as money going out.
- Active recurring invoices and your latest approved payroll run.

## Read the chart

The line shows the expected balance week by week. A dip below zero warns you early so you can chase a customer or delay a payment.

## Try scenarios

Add one-off amounts, such as a planned purchase or a loan repayment, and compare the result with the base forecast. Scenarios are for planning only and change nothing in your books.

## Good to know

- A forecast is only as good as your due dates. Keep invoices and bills up to date.
- It starts from your reconciled bank balance. See [Bank reconciliation](/help/bank-reconciliation).

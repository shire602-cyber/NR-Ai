[Receipts](/receipts) turns paper into expenses.

## Add receipts

- **On a phone**, choose **Take photo** to open the camera, or pick pictures from your gallery.
- **On a computer**, drag images or PDFs onto the page. You can add many at once.

## Review

Muhasib.ai reads the supplier, date, total and VAT. Check each field, correct anything that is wrong and choose the expense account.

## Save

Saving posts the expense with its VAT, and keeps the image attached as evidence. Receipts with a valid tax invoice support your input VAT claim.

## Good to know

- Reading is a suggestion. You always confirm before anything is posted.
- Large photos are reduced to a smaller size before upload, so they are quick on mobile data.
- Keep receipts for at least five years. They stay in your document vault.

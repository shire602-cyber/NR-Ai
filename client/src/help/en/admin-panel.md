The **Admin** section is for platform administrators only. If you do not see it, your account is not an administrator account.

## What is in it

- **Dashboard** summarises clients, documents and activity.
- **Clients** and **Client documents** let staff manage client companies and their uploaded files.
- **User invitations** invites client users and portal users.
- **Client import** brings clients in from a file.
- **User management** lists users and their status.
- **Activity logs** records administrative actions.

## Good to know

- Administrator access is powerful. Grant it to as few people as possible and protect those accounts with two-step verification. See [Account security](/help/security).
- Every administrative action is logged.

[Reports](/reports) is the home of your statements and analysis.

## Core statements

- **Profit and loss** over a date range.
- **Balance sheet** as at a date.
- **Trial balance**, to check that debits equal credits.
- **Aged receivables and payables**, to see who owes what and for how long.

## Run a report

Pick a report, set the dates, and run it. Click an amount to drill down to the documents behind it.

## Export and share

Download as PDF in English or Arabic, or as CSV or Excel for further analysis.

## Schedule delivery

Use [Report schedules](/reports/schedules) to email a report to yourself or your accountant every week or month.

## Good to know

- Reports use posted entries only.
- Each figure traces back to the ledger, so you can answer any question about it.

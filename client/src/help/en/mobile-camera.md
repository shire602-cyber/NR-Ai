Muhasib.ai works in your phone's browser and adapts to small screens. There is no separate app to download.

## Install it

In your browser, choose **Add to Home screen**. It opens full-screen like an app.

## Photograph receipts

Open [Receipts](/receipts) and choose **Take photo**. Your phone opens its camera. Photos are reduced in size before upload, so they send quickly on mobile data. Review what was read, then save.

## Arabic and English

Switch language from the toggle in the menu or on the sign-in page. The layout flips to right-to-left for Arabic.

## Offline

If your connection drops, a banner tells you. Changes you make are saved and sent when you reconnect.

## Good to know

- Tables scroll sideways on small screens, and buttons are sized for touch.
- Sign out on shared devices.

Accounting firms get the **Firm** workspace for managing client companies.

## What is in it

- **Command centre** shows what needs attention across all clients.
- **Clients** lists every client company. Switch into one to work in its books.
- **Staff** manages your team and which clients each person can see.
- **Health** scores clients on the state of their records and deadlines.
- **Communications** and **Document chasing** ask clients for missing documents and track replies.
- **Email intake** turns documents emailed to the firm into items to file.

## Access

Firm owners see all clients. Firm admins see only the clients assigned to them.

## Good to know

- Deleting a client company needs the firm owner's confirmation. See [Export and delete your data](/help/data-export-deletion).

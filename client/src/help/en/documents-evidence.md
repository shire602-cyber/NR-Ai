UAE rules require you to keep your records for five years. Muhasib.ai keeps the documents with the transactions they support.

## Document vault

[Document vault](/document-vault) stores contracts, licences, statements and other files. Upload, tag and search them. Receipts you scan are kept with the expense.

## Evidence centre

[Evidence centre](/evidence-center) checks that your VAT claims have the support they need, such as a valid tax invoice, and lists what is missing.

## Tax return archive

[Tax return archive](/tax-return-archive) keeps the returns you finalised, with the figures as filed.

## Versions

[Document versions](/document-versions) shows earlier versions of a file, so you can see what changed.

## Good to know

- Accounting records (journals, invoices, bills, VAT filings) are kept read-only for the retention period even if a company is deleted. See [Export and delete your data](/help/data-export-deletion).
- Export everything at any time as a ZIP file.

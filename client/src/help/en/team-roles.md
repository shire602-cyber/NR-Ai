Add colleagues and your accountant in [Team](/team).

## Invite someone

Choose **Invite**, enter their email and pick a role. They receive an email to join the company.

## Roles

- **Owner** has full control, including approval rules, security and deleting the company.
- **Accountant** and **CFO** work with the books, reports and filings.
- **Employee** sees **only their own records**: their expense claims, payslips and leave. An employee cannot open the company's invoices, bills, ledgers or reports.

## Change or remove access

Change a person's role or remove them at any time. A removed person loses access immediately. The company owner cannot be removed.

## Good to know

- Give people the least access they need.
- Everything people do is recorded in the activity history.
- Your plan sets how many users you can invite. See [Plans and billing](/help/subscription).

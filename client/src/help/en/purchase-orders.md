A purchase order records what you have asked a vendor to supply. It posts nothing to your books by itself.

## Create an order

1. Open [Purchase orders](/purchase-orders) and choose **New order**.
2. Choose the vendor, add the items (products), quantities and prices.
3. Send the order to the vendor.

## When the goods arrive

Convert the order to a bill. Lines, prices and VAT carry across, so you only check them against the vendor's invoice. Lines with a product add stock; if you receive goods before the bill, they wait in goods received not invoiced (2015) until the bill clears it. See [Inventory](/help/inventory).

## Good to know

- Approval rules can apply to orders above a threshold. See [Approvals](/help/approvals).

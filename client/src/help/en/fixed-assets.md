[Fixed assets](/fixed-assets) keeps the register of what the company owns for more than a year.

## Add an asset

Enter the name, purchase date, cost and useful life. The asset starts depreciating from its purchase date.

An asset is **in the books** when it is tied to the purchase that put its cost on a fixed-asset account: create it **from a bill line**, or open the register and **link** it to the posted bill or journal entry that bought it. Linking posts nothing, so the cost is never counted twice. Assets not linked yet are listed apart on the register with a warning and are left out of its totals, so the register can tie to accounts 1290 and 1240. An asset bought in a **closed year** can be registered too: nothing is posted into that year, and the screen says so.

## Depreciation

Depreciation is posted to the accumulated depreciation account, not directly to the asset, so the cost stays visible on the balance sheet. Review the schedule to see each period's charge.

## Catch-up and closed periods

If you add an asset late, or skip a run, use **catch-up** to post the missed months. Months that fall in a **closed or locked period** are not posted back into that period: the whole missed amount is posted in the **first open period** instead, dated there, so your locked figures never change. The register shows how much was caught up.

## Dispose of an asset

Record a sale or write-off with the date, the price **without VAT**, the buyer and the VAT treatment. **Depreciation runs up to and including the disposal date**: whole months before it in full, the disposal month pro rata by days (a disposal on the 15th of a 30-day month charges half that month). Depreciation already posted beyond the date is reversed. The gain or loss is measured against the net book value at the disposal date.

A sale that is a supply (standard-rated 5%, zero-rated or exempt) needs a tax invoice: Muhasib raises it to the buyer, so the output VAT reaches your VAT 201 under the buyer's emirate, and the sale is not counted as revenue. Choose the bank account the money arrived in to settle the invoice at once, or leave it open and match the bank line later. A disposal date in a locked period is refused.

## Good to know

- The register shows cost, accumulated depreciation and net book value for every asset.

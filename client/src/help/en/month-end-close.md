[Month-end close](/month-end) guides you through finishing a period.

## Month-end close

Typical steps: reconcile the bank, record outstanding bills and expenses, post depreciation and payroll, review the trial balance and check VAT. Then **lock the period**.

**A month-end close posts no closing entries.** Revenue and expense stay in your profit and loss, so September's report still shows September's revenue after you lock it. Locking only stops anyone from posting, editing or voiding documents dated inside the month.

## Year-end close

Closing the year is a separate step. Only at year end are the year's income and expenses moved into retained earnings (3020), dated the last day of the fiscal year. Do it after the year's last month is locked and your accountant has reviewed the figures. Folding profit into equity removes it from the profit and loss, so it is not done monthly.

## Good to know

- Locking protects figures you gave your accountant or put on a VAT return.
- Documents dated inside a filed VAT period are protected even without a lock. See [VAT returns](/help/vat-filing).
- You can still post into later periods while an earlier one is locked.
- Depreciation for a closed period is handled in [Fixed assets](/help/fixed-assets).

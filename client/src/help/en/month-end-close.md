[Month-end close](/month-end) guides you through finishing a period.

## The checklist

Typical steps: reconcile the bank, record outstanding bills and expenses, post depreciation and payroll, review the trial balance and check VAT.

## Lock the period

When you are happy, lock the period. Nobody can post, edit or void documents dated inside it. If you must change something, unlock the period first.

## Good to know

- Locking protects the figures you gave to your accountant or put on a VAT return.
- Documents dated inside a VAT period that has been filed are protected even without a lock. See [VAT filing](/help/vat-filing).
- You can still post into later periods while an earlier one is locked.

Muhasib.ai keeps your books in UAE dirhams with VAT built in. These steps take about fifteen minutes.

## 1. Add your company details

Open [Company settings](/settings/company) and enter the legal name, trade licence, emirate, **TRN** (tax registration number), fiscal year and base currency. Your TRN and name print on every tax invoice.

## 2. Check the chart of accounts

A UAE chart of accounts is created for you. Open [Chart of accounts](/chart-of-accounts) to rename accounts or add your own. See [Chart of accounts](/help/chart-of-accounts).

## 3. Bring your opening position

Moving from another system? Use [Import data](/import), then post [Opening balances](/opening-balances). Starting fresh? Skip this step.

## 4. Invite your team

Add colleagues and your accountant in [Team](/team) and give each the right role. See [Team and roles](/help/team-roles).

## 5. Send your first invoice

Go to [Invoices](/invoices), add a customer and a line, and issue it. VAT is calculated for you.

> The dashboard shows what needs attention: overdue invoices, bills due and upcoming VAT deadlines.

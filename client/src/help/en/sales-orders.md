A sales order records an order you have agreed to fill. It posts nothing to your books until you invoice it.

## Create an order

Open [Sales orders](/sales-orders) and create one, or convert an accepted [quote](/help/quotes) into a sales order. Lines, prices and VAT carry across.

## Invoice in parts

Create an invoice for the whole order, or for part of it, as you deliver. The order keeps track of what has been invoiced and what is still open.

## Good to know

- An order shows the stock you have for each item as a guide. It does not reserve the stock.
- Invoices created from an order follow the normal posting and VAT rules. See [Create and send VAT invoices](/help/invoices).

An invoice records what a customer owes you. When you issue it, Muhasib.ai posts the revenue and the output VAT to your books.

## Create an invoice

1. Open [Invoices](/invoices) and choose **New invoice**.
2. Pick a customer, set the date and due date, and add lines with a quantity, price and VAT rate.
3. Save it as a draft, or issue it. A draft posts nothing.

## Issue and send

Issuing gives the invoice its number and posts it. Download the PDF in English, Arabic or both, or email it to the customer with a link they can open.

## Record payments and status

Record a payment with the date, amount and bank account. Partial payments are supported. The status is **derived from the payments and credit notes**, not set by hand: an invoice is sent until money arrives, then partially paid, then paid. Deleting or reversing a payment moves the status back.

## Corrections

An issued invoice is not edited. To correct it, issue a [credit note](/help/credit-notes) from the invoice, for all of it or for selected lines, and issue a new invoice if needed. This keeps your VAT return and your numbering clean.

## Good to know

- Invoice numbers run in sequence. A gap needs an explanation, so void rather than delete.
- Locked periods and filed VAT periods cannot receive new invoices dated inside them.
- Customers who pay in advance: see [Customer advances](/help/customer-advances).

Muhasib.ai includes AI tools that save time on routine work.

## What they do

- **AI CFO** answers questions about your numbers in plain language.
- **Categorisation** suggests an account for a transaction.
- **Anomaly detection** points out unusual entries, such as a duplicate bill or an unexpected amount.
- **AI inbox** reads documents you send in and prepares them for review.

## You decide

AI output is a suggestion. Nothing is posted until you review and accept it, and you can always change the account or amount.

## When AI is off

AI features need a provider key. If none is configured for your environment, the page says so, and the rest of the product works as normal.

## Good to know

- Check amounts, dates and VAT before you accept a suggestion.
- Do not put passwords or card numbers in a question.

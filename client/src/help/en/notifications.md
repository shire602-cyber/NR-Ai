Muhasib.ai tells you when something needs attention: overdue invoices, bills due, approvals waiting, deadlines close.

## Where you see them

- The bell in the header lists recent notifications. Open [Notifications](/notifications) to see them all.
- [Reminders](/reminders) shows the reminders you set.

## Choose how

Open [Notification preferences](/notification-preferences) to turn each kind on or off, and to choose email or in-app.

## Good to know

- Email needs an email provider to be configured for your account. In-app notifications always work.
- Security alerts, such as a sign-in from a new device, are sent to your email address when email is available.

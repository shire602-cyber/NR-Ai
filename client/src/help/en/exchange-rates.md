Your books are kept in AED. Some documents and bank accounts can be in another currency, and are converted at the rate you set.

## Set rates

Open [Exchange rates](/exchange-rates) and enter the rate for each currency and date. The rate for a document's date is used when you create it.

## Foreign-currency bills and bank accounts

Choose the currency on a bill. The bill shows its own currency, and the ledger posts the dirham value. A USD bank account is reconciled in USD. See [Bank reconciliation](/help/bank-reconciliation).

## Payments and differences

When a payment is made at a different rate, the difference is posted as an exchange gain or loss.

## Good to know

- A foreign-currency document needs a rate for its date. Without one, you are asked to add it.
- The AED is pegged to the US dollar, so USD amounts are converted at the fixed rate.
- Customer advances are recorded in AED only. See [Customer advances](/help/customer-advances).
- Imported open invoices and bills in other currencies need an exchange rate in the file.

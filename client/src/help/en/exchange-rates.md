Your books are kept in AED. Documents can be in another currency, and are converted at the rate you set.

## Set rates

Open [Exchange rates](/exchange-rates) and enter the rate for each currency and date. The rate for a document's date is used when you create it.

## Foreign-currency documents

Choose the currency on an invoice or bill. The document shows its own currency, and the ledger posts the dirham value.

## Payments and differences

When a payment arrives at a different rate, the difference is posted as an exchange gain or loss.

## Good to know

- A document in a foreign currency needs a rate for its date. Without one, you are asked to add it.
- The AED is pegged to the US dollar, so USD documents are converted at the fixed rate.
- Imported open invoices and bills in other currencies need an exchange rate in the file.

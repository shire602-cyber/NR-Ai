[Sales settings](/settings/sales) groups the options that shape your quotes, orders and invoices.

## Price lists

Create a price list for a group of customers, such as wholesale. Choose it on a document and the item prices follow the list.

## Custom fields

Add your own fields, such as a purchase order number or a project code. Choose which documents they appear on, and whether they print on the PDF.

## Online payments

If an online payment provider is set up for your company, customers can pay an invoice from its link, and the payment is recorded against the invoice. If no provider is configured, the page says so and the invoice shows no payment button.

## Good to know

- Price lists and custom fields change what you enter, not how VAT is calculated.
- Payment provider fees and settlement are handled by the provider. Ask your accountant how to treat gateway fees for VAT.

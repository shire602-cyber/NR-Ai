Opening balances put your accounts, open invoices and open bills in place on the day before you start using Muhasib.ai.

## Two ways to do it

- **Import wizard.** Upload the trial balance, open invoices and open bills exported from your old system. See [Import data](/help/import-data).
- **Opening balances page.** Enter or paste the balances yourself in [Opening balances](/opening-balances).

## What happens when you post

- One balanced journal entry is created, dated the day before go-live.
- Any difference goes to **Opening balance equity**, so the books balance from day one.
- Open invoices and bills are recorded as open items only. They post no revenue and no VAT again, because that was already reported in your old system.

## Before you post

1. Preview the entry and check the totals.
2. Make sure receivables and payables on the trial balance match the open items you listed.
3. Post it once. You can reverse it from the same page if you find a mistake.

> Closed (fully paid) invoices and bills are not imported. Keep your old system's reports for the years before go-live.

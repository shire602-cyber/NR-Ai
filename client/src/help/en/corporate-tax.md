[Corporate tax](/corporate-tax) estimates the tax due on your taxable income.

## How it is calculated

UAE corporate tax is 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% on the part above it. Muhasib.ai starts from the profit in your ledger for the tax period, applies the adjustments you enter and shows the tax payable.

## Adjustments: add-backs and deductions

Enter **add-backs** (expenses the law does not allow, such as non-deductible entertainment or fines) to increase taxable income, and **deductions** to reduce it. Each line has a description, so the workpaper explains how the figure was reached.

## Small business relief

If you qualify, you can **elect small business relief** for the period. The computation then shows the relief and taxable income of nil. The election is yours to make and has conditions on revenue and on eligibility: confirm it with your tax adviser before you elect.

## Use it

1. Choose the tax period.
2. Review the profit taken from your books.
3. Enter adjustments and, if eligible, the election.
4. Save the workpaper for your accountant.

## Good to know

- The estimate is only as good as your books. Reconcile and close the period first. See [Month-end and year-end close](/help/month-end-close).
- Whether you are a taxable person, a qualifying free zone person or exempt depends on your facts.
- Corporate tax returns are filed on the FTA portal.

[Corporate tax](/corporate-tax) estimates the tax due on your taxable income.

## How it is calculated

UAE corporate tax is 9% on taxable income above AED 375,000. Muhasib.ai starts from the profit in your ledger for the tax period, applies the adjustments you enter and shows the tax payable.

## Use it

1. Choose the tax period.
2. Review the profit taken from your books.
3. Enter adjustments, such as non-deductible expenses.
4. Save the workpaper, so your accountant can review how the figure was reached.

## Good to know

- The estimate is only as good as your books. Reconcile and close the period first. See [Month-end close](/help/month-end-close).
- Whether you are a taxable person, a qualifying free zone person or exempt depends on your facts. Confirm with your tax adviser.
- Corporate tax returns are filed on the FTA portal.

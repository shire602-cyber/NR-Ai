Expense claims let staff recover money they spent on the business.

## Submit a claim

1. Open [Expense claims](/expense-claims) and choose **New claim**.
2. Add each expense with the date, category, amount and VAT, and attach the receipt.
3. Submit the claim for approval.

## Review and pay

An approver reviews each line and approves or rejects the claim. Approving posts the expenses and the VAT you can reclaim. Then record the reimbursement to the employee.

## Good to know

- Add a receipt for every line. VAT can only be reclaimed with a valid tax invoice.
- Claims can be split across projects. See [Projects and time](/help/projects).
- Approval rules can require more than one approver. See [Approvals](/help/approvals).

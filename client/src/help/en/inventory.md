[Inventory](/inventory) is your list of items. Invoices and bills pick lines from it.

## Items and services

Each item has a name, optional SKU, selling price, purchase cost and VAT rate. Mark an item as a service when there is no stock to track.

## Track stock

For stocked items, buying adds quantity and selling removes it. When you sell, the cost of the goods sold is posted for you from the stock cost, alongside the revenue.

## Adjust stock

Count your stock and enter the difference as an adjustment. The change posts to your books with the reason you give.

## Import

Bring products from another system with [Import data](/help/import-data). Items match by SKU, or by name, so repeating an import does not create copies.

## Good to know

- You cannot sell more than you hold. If the on-hand figure is wrong, record a stock adjustment first.
- Item prices on an invoice can be changed per line without changing the item.

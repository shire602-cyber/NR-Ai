Open [Security](/settings/security).

## Turn on two-step verification

1. Choose **Set up two-step verification**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password. You can type the key by hand instead.
3. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.
4. **Save your 10 recovery codes.** They are shown once. Each works one time if you lose your phone.

From then on, signing in asks for a code after your password.

## Lost your phone?

On the sign-in code screen choose **Use a recovery code instead**. Then generate new codes from Security.

## Devices you are signed in on

The list shows each device, its address and when it was last active. Sign out any you do not recognise, or sign out all other devices at once.

## Change your password

Enter your current password, a new one and, if two-step verification is on, a code. Your other devices are signed out.

## Require two-step verification for the company

Company owners can require it for everyone with the owner, accountant or CFO role. People without it are taken to the setup page when they sign in.

## Good to know

- Too many wrong codes pause attempts for a minute.
- We email you when your account is used from a new device, when email is available.
- Turning off two-step verification needs your password and a code, and is blocked while a company requires it.

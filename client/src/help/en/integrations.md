[Integrations](/integrations) lists the services you can connect, such as exporting invoices and reports to Google Sheets.

## Connect a service

Open the service and follow the steps. Some need credentials from the provider.

## When something says "not configured"

A connection that needs provider keys stays off until they are added. The page says **not configured** instead of pretending to work. Ask your administrator or contact support to enable it.

## Build your own

Developers can connect their own software through the REST API and signed webhooks. See [API keys and webhooks](/help/developers-api).

Most entries are created for you by invoices, bills, payments and payroll. Use [Journal](/journal) for adjustments that have no document, such as accruals or corrections.

## Create an entry

1. Choose **New entry** and set the date and a description.
2. Add lines, each with an account and a debit or credit amount.
3. The entry must balance: total debits equal total credits.
4. Save as a draft, or post it.

## System entries are read-only

Entries made by invoices, bills, payments, payroll, depreciation and opening balances show their source and cannot be edited here. Change the document instead.

## Correct a mistake

Reverse a posted entry. The reversal is a new entry with the debits and credits swapped, so the history stays complete.

## Good to know

- You cannot post into a locked period or a VAT period that has been filed.
- Approval rules can require a second person to approve manual entries. See [Approvals](/help/approvals).
- Click any entry to see who created it and when.

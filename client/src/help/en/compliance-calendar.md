[Compliance calendar](/compliance-calendar) lists the dates that matter: VAT return and payment deadlines, corporate tax dates and tasks you add.

## Use it

- Switch between month and list views.
- Open a deadline to see what is needed and jump to the right page.
- Add your own tasks, such as renewing a trade licence.

## Reminders

Deadlines appear on your dashboard when they are close. Choose how you want to be reminded in [Notification preferences](/help/notifications).

## Good to know

- Check dates against the FTA's published deadlines for your registration. Penalties for late filing apply.

[Company settings](/settings/company) holds the details that appear on your documents and drive your books.

## Legal details

Legal name in English and Arabic, trade licence, emirate, address and contact details. These print on invoices and quotes.

## Tax

Your **TRN**, VAT registration status and filing frequency, as registered with the FTA.

## Books

Fiscal year start, base currency and defaults such as payment terms and the next invoice number.

## Good to know

- Changes apply to new documents. Documents already issued keep the details they were issued with.
- Company-wide security options, including requiring two-step verification, are in [Security](/help/security).

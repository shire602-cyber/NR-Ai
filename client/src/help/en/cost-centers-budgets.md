## Cost centres

[Cost centres](/cost-centers) let you tag income and expenses by department, branch or project. Allocate lines to a centre when you post, then read a profit and loss for each centre.

## Budgets

[Budgets](/budgets) store a plan: a monthly amount for each account or category.

1. Create a budget plan for the year.
2. Add lines with monthly amounts and link each to an account.
3. Open the variance view to compare the budget with actual journal entries. Green is under budget, red is over.

## Good to know

- Variance uses posted entries only. Drafts are not counted.
- A budget is a plan, not a limit: nothing stops you spending above it.

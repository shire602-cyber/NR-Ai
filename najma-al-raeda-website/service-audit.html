Questions

Frequently asked.

Do free-zone companies need an audit?

Many free zones require audited financial statements annually. We confirm your obligation and get you ready.

Can you fix our books before an audit?

Yes — audit-readiness and clean-up is one of our most requested services.

Do you perform the statutory audit yourselves?

We prepare you for audit and liaise with registered auditors; for independence, the statutory audit is performed by a separate registered audit firm.

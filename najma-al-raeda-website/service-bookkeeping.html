Questions

Frequently asked.

How quickly can you take over our books?

Usually within one to two weeks. We migrate your data, reconcile opening balances and agree a monthly cadence.

Which software do you use?

We work with Zoho Books, Xero and QuickBooks. If you have no system yet, we’ll recommend the best fit for your size and sector.

Do we get a dedicated person?

Yes. Every client has a named lead accountant who knows your business — not a rotating call centre.

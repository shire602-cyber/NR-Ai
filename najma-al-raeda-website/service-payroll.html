Questions

Frequently asked.

What is WPS?

The Wage Protection System is the UAE’s mandatory salary-transfer mechanism. Non-compliance can affect your visas and licence — we keep you compliant.

Can you handle end-of-service calculations?

Yes — gratuity is calculated accurately to UAE labour law whenever an employee leaves.

How do we send you payroll changes?

A simple monthly process — you tell us joiners, leavers and changes, and we handle the rest.

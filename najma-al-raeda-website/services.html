Our services

Full-spectrum financial care for UAE businesses.

Whether you need a clean set of books, watertight tax compliance, or a finance leader in your corner, every engagement is scoped to your stage and delivered by a team that knows your name.

01

Accounting & Bookkeeping →

Real-time, IFRS-compliant books maintained by a dedicated accountant, with monthly management reports that turn raw transactions into decisions.

  • Day-to-day bookkeeping & reconciliation
  • Monthly management accounts
  • Accounts payable & receivable
  • Cloud accounting setup (Zoho, Xero, QuickBooks)
  • Chart of accounts design
  • Year-end financial statements
02

VAT & Corporate Tax →

End-to-end VAT and UAE Corporate Tax handled by FTA-registered specialists — from first registration to every filing deadline, with zero penalties as the standard.

  • VAT registration & de-registration
  • VAT return preparation & filing
  • UAE Corporate Tax registration
  • Corporate Tax computation & filing
  • Tax health checks & advisory
  • FTA audit support & representation
03

Audit & Assurance →

Independent audit and assurance that gives regulators, banks, investors and shareholders complete confidence in your numbers.

  • Statutory & external audit liaison
  • Internal audit & controls review
  • Audit readiness & clean-up
  • Free-zone compliance audits
  • Due diligence support
  • Agreed-upon procedures
04

CFO & Advisory →

Senior financial leadership without the full-time cost. We sit beside you for budgeting, forecasting, fundraising and the decisions that shape your next phase.

  • Fractional / outsourced CFO
  • Budgeting & cash-flow forecasting
  • Financial modelling & KPIs
  • Fundraising & investor reporting
  • Profitability & pricing analysis
  • Board & management reporting
05

Company Formation →

A clean foundation for your UAE entity — mainland or free zone — with licensing, structuring and banking handled so you can trade from day one.

  • Mainland & free-zone setup
  • Trade licence & activity selection
  • Corporate structuring
  • Bank account opening support
  • PRO & visa coordination
  • Ongoing compliance calendar
06

Payroll & WPS →

Accurate, on-time payroll fully compliant with UAE Wage Protection System rules — so your team is paid right, every month.

  • Monthly payroll processing
  • WPS file generation & submission
  • Payslip & record management
  • End-of-service gratuity calculation
  • Leave & benefits tracking
  • Payroll compliance reviews

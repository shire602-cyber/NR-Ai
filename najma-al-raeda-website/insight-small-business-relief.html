Small Business Relief: could your company pay 0% corporate tax?

By the Najma Al Raeda tax team26 May 20264 min read

One of the most valuable — and most overlooked — features of the UAE Corporate Tax regime is Small Business Relief. For genuinely small businesses it can reduce the Corporate Tax bill to nil and dramatically simplify compliance.

What the relief does

If your business’s revenue is at or below AED 3 million in the relevant tax period (and in all previous periods since the regime began), you can elect to be treated as having no taxable income for that period. In practice that means no Corporate Tax to pay and a simplified compliance path.

The conditions

  • It applies to resident persons whose revenue stays at or below the AED 3 million ceiling in the current and all prior relevant periods.
  • It is an election — you must claim it in your return; it is not applied automatically.
  • It is currently available for tax periods up to the end of 2026 under the relevant Ministerial Decision.
  • Qualifying Free Zone Persons and members of large multinational groups are excluded.

Worth weighing up: electing for relief means you generally cannot carry forward tax losses or certain disallowed expenditure from that period. For most micro-businesses the simplicity is worth it — but if you are investing heavily and generating losses you want to carry forward, it is worth modelling both options. We do this for clients before the election is made.

Should you elect?

For the great majority of early-stage UAE businesses under the threshold, Small Business Relief is a straightforward win: no tax, less paperwork. The key is to confirm you genuinely meet the revenue condition for every relevant period and to make the election correctly in your return.

This article is general guidance based on UAE Federal Tax Authority rules current at the time of writing and does not constitute formal tax advice. Rules, thresholds and deadlines change — always confirm the current position with the FTA or speak to our team before acting.

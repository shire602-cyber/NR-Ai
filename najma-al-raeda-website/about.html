Our story

Built in Dubai, for the businesses that build Dubai.

Najma Al Raeda began in 2017 with a simple conviction: that growing businesses deserve accounting that is precise, proactive and genuinely human. Nine years on, that conviction still runs through everything we do.

Who we are

A boutique firm with institutional rigour.

We started as a two-person practice above a Deira trading office, helping a handful of SMEs make sense of their books. As the UAE introduced VAT in 2018 and Corporate Tax in 2023, businesses needed more than a bookkeeper — they needed a partner who could keep them compliant and confident through constant change.

Today, Najma Al Raeda has served more than 700 businesses and supports over 500 active clients across every emirate. We've grown deliberately, keeping the close, partner-led relationships that defined us from day one — never trading attention for scale.

What we stand for

Three principles, never compromised.

01

Precision

Numbers are either right or they're not. We treat accuracy as non-negotiable and reconcile to the last fil.

02

Proactivity

We flag the deadline, the risk and the opportunity before you have to ask. No client should ever be caught off guard.

03

Partnership

We win when you grow. That alignment shapes every recommendation we make and every relationship we keep.

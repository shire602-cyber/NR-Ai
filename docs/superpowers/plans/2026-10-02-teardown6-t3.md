Independent accountant review, 2 October 2026. I used only the running app (port 5095), the help centre, the public OpenAPI file and the PDFs/files the app produced.

## Findings, by severity

### Money

1. **The mid-month joiner is paid for months before he joined, and is never pro-rated. Critical.**
   - Steps: add Ahmed with a join date of 2026-08-15. Create and calculate the July run.
   - Result: Ahmed gets 9,000 in July, plus a 350 gratuity accrual. In August he gets 9,000 instead of 4,935.48 (17/31 days); the SIF says 31 days; accrual 350 instead of 191.94. The run shows `warnings: []`.
   - There is no "days worked" input. The only workaround is a manual deduction, which runs into finding 3.
   - Verdict: the product is wrong.
2. **Final settlement ignores the opening EOSB provision and charges the gratuity to expense a second time. High.**
   - Steps: post the opening 2036 balance, run three months, then Final settlement → New → Maria → 30 Sep.
   - Result: "Provision used" defaults to "Accrued in payroll 875.01", although the screen itself shows "Provision balance in the ledger 92,683.33". The 11,370.20 true-up is charged to 5028, and 11,375 stays in 2036 for good.
   - Every client moving in with existing staff will hit this. It is only right if the bookkeeper knows to type the figure into "Provision used (optional)".
3. **Manual deductions post to 2034 Payroll Deductions Payable. High.**
   - My pro-rata and absence corrections (4,731.19) went to 2034, a liability, instead of reducing 5020.
   - Result: a liability that does not exist, and salary expense overstated. "Edit overtime/deductions" has no type (absence, fine, third-party deduction).
4. **Unpaid leave and sick half-pay are deducted on basic salary only. High.**
   - August unpaid leave: deducted 4,000/30 × 10 = 1,333.33. Expected 6,000/30 × 10 = 2,000, so the employee was overpaid 666.67.
   - September tier-2 sick leave: deducted 333.33. Expected 500 (Art. 31 refers to wage, not basic), so 166.67 overpaid.
   - The 30-day basis and the 30-day cap are applied correctly.
5. **Gratuity service ends the day before the last working day.** The termination date is excluded every time, for example Maria at 12,245.21 against 12,250.00. The amounts are small but systematic, and they put every settlement slightly below MOHRE's figure. **Medium.**
6. **No leave-pay provision, and leave balances that are invented. Medium-high.**
   - There is no leave-pay accrual (about 56,767 unrecorded at 30 Sep), so the 8,750 encashment went straight to 5020.
   - Balances open with a 30-day carry-forward nobody entered.
   - Fatima's first-year accrual is one month late (10 against 12).
   - Accrual counts the current month in advance (25 days on 2 Oct; 22.5 is right).
   - Unpaid days do not reduce service or accrual.
7. **The gratuity cap is "2 years total salary"** (288,000 for Ravi). Most practitioners cap at two years of basic (192,000). Disputed, but it should at least be a setting. **Low–medium.**

### Compliance and controls

8. **The employee role can see and change payroll. Critical.**
   - I signed in as Maria (role "Employee – Submit expenses only"). The payroll page says "يمكنك رؤية سجلاتك الخاصة فقط" (you can see only your own records), then lists every colleague's total salary.
   - It also shows Fatima's sick leave, loans and settlements.
   - Through the app's own endpoints she could: read all employees with IBANs, the items of every run, every payslip PDF (I downloaded Fatima's), the SIF file and the trial balance.
   - She could also change her own salary (PATCH /employees 200), create a November payroll run, recalculate the October run, and run the gratuity calculator on any colleague.
   - This is a data-protection breach and a route to fraud. The release notes disclose only "read leave balances and loans".
9. **The person who prepares a run can approve it. High.**
   - The owner created and calculated July, clicked Approve, and it posted.
   - The release notes say "nobody can approve their own document". The rule dialog only blocks creators of "a claim or journal".
   - The approval queue's FROM column is blank, and the history does not show who prepared the run.
10. **The SIF file would not pass a bank or MOHRE check. High.**
    - It is fixed-width text with the SCR header first; the standard is comma-separated with SCR last.
    - The employer establishment ID and bank code are blank, and the file was produced without any warning.
    - The person ID is the 8-character labour card number, not the 14-digit MOHRE person code. There is no field for one.
    - Dates are YYYYMMDD. It has a deductions column. Days on leave are not reported (Fatima: 10 unpaid days, 5 sick days). Ahmed shows 31 days in August.
    - IBANs and routing codes are not validated.
11. **"Approve" marks every item Paid, but no money has moved.** The run has no "record payment" step, although the help article says "After the bank pays, record the payment". I cleared 2030 with a manual journal. **Medium.**
12. **The payroll register does not tie or add up. Medium.**
    - "All payroll runs" includes the unapproved October run, giving a total net of 237,502.15 against 179,602.15 in the ledger.
    - The Deductions column leaves out pension, leave and loan deductions. Khalid's row reads 15,000 + 7,000 − 0 = 20,900.
    - The "Payroll register" button on the run page does nothing.
13. **Final settlement is a dead end. Medium.**
    - Post uses a native confirm, then fails silently: 409 "A payroll run is waiting to deduct a loan instalment".
    - The blocking October run cannot be deleted, and rejecting it in Approvals does not release it.
    - Workaround: make Maria inactive, recalculate October, make her active again, then post. Posting while she is inactive returns 409 EMPLOYEE_NOT_ACTIVE.
    - Pay with a future date also fails silently.
    - There is no settlement statement or PDF for the employee to sign. The settlement is not covered by approval rules.
14. **Payroll runs cannot be deleted.** The November run the employee created stays for good. **Low–medium.**
15. **Invited members cannot sign in when email is not configured.** The invite creates the user with an unknown password, and there is no copyable link. **Medium**, but it depends on the environment.
16. **Dates shift by a day.** A join date picked in the form is stored as the previous day at 20:00Z. Journal numbers carry the previous day: JE-20260629-001 is dated 30 Jun. **Low.**

### Usability, Arabic and 375 px

17. **The employee form is missing fields.** There is no contract type, Emirates ID / MOHRE person code, gender, probation or pension flag. Number fields are pre-filled with 0, so typing gives "015000".
18. **Arabic gaps.**
    - Approval history shows raw codes: `payroll_run`, `approved 1/1`, `owner`. Its "Total" adds rejected and approved amounts together.
    - Status "terminated" is not translated.
    - Run tables use English names although the Arabic names are stored.
    - "6 موظفينالصافي" is missing a space (English shows "6 employeesNet").
    - A full stop jumps to the start of the line in the rules note.
19. **At 375 px (Arabic)**, the Download SIF button sits off-screen in a row that scrolls sideways with no cue. The approval-rules actions are off-screen as well. The settlement and leave screens are fine.
20. **Reports.**
    - Leave balances show the year as "2,026" and give men maternity balances.
    - The loan report does not add up: 1,200 deducted + 0 outstanding against a principal of 12,000, with the settlement recovery not shown.
    - The reports hub is full of jargon ("Packs2Comps3Starters2", "autopilot").
21. **Payslips.** They are bilingual and clean. "Pay Date" is the date the run was created (2 Oct), not the payment date. There are no period dates and no leave or loan balance.
22. **Wording and small slips.**
    - The gratuity calculator label reads "( yrs x 21 days)", with the number missing.
    - The settlement text "gratuity less the provision already accrued" reads to staff as though their gratuity is reduced.
    - The help article does not cover leave, loans or settlement.

## Sign-off

**VAT return for Q3: WOULD SIGN (nil).** This scenario has no taxable supplies or recoverable inputs, and the return is nil. Payroll correctly stays out of VAT.

**Year-end: WOULD NOT SIGN.**
- **Salary cost and liabilities.** Left alone, the product overstates salary cost by about 13,900 in this quarter alone (9,000 + 4,064.52 + 666.67 + 166.67).
- **Gratuity, double-counted.** Its default settlement would have expensed 11,370 of gratuity a second time.
- **Gratuity provision.** 11,375 would sit in 2036 for good.
- **Unrecorded leave liability.** About 56,767 of leave liability is not on the balance sheet.
- **Fake liability.** 2034 holds 4,731.19 that nobody is owed.
- **The register.** It does not agree with the ledger.
- **Controls.** The person who prepares a run can approve it. Any employee can read, and alter, payroll master data.

## Competitor remark

The ledger design is better than most local tools. Pension at 5% / 12.5%, the GCC exclusion, the 21/30-day accrual with the five-year switch, loans through 1080 and the provision report are all thought through, and the posted runs tie to 2030 and the bank. Today, though, I would not move a Zoho Books or QuickBooks client with staff onto it, and Wafeq's payroll is ahead on WPS. The reasons are finding 8 (employees can see and change everyone's pay), finding 10 (a SIF the bank will not accept), finding 1 (no pro-rata) and finding 2 (the settlement double-counts). Each of these is a reason on its own. Fix those four, then make "Approve ≠ Paid" and the register footing right, and this becomes the better choice for a UAE SME with 5–50 staff.

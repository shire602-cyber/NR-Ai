# Teardown 7 (verification after fixes): v1, Sharjah inventory trader

Independent UAE accountant, no code access. I used only the running app at http://v1.localhost:5101 (port 5101, database `muhasib_p9_v1`), the in-app help centre, the OpenAPI document and the PDFs the app produced. I did every transaction through the screens. I did not use the public API. Roughly 40% of the work was done in Arabic: expenses, the refund, contacts, month-end close, VAT filing and Autopilot, and the VAT audit reports.

## Scenario

**Company.** Al Qasimi Building Materials LLC, Sharjah. VAT-registered, filing quarterly, financial year = calendar year. Inventory costing is switched on (Settings > Company > "Post inventory to ledger (COGS)"). There is one item: CEM-50, a 50 kg cement bag.

**Opening, 1 Jul 2026:** 200 bags at 18; receivable Al Noor 12,600; payable Gulf Cement 8,400; bank 50,000; share capital 57,800.

**Q3:** bills 3 Jul (Gulf, 500 at 20) and 6 Jul (Ras, 300 at 22); 15 Jul Dubai sale (Emirates Towers, 400 at 35, 5% line discount, AED 200 shipping); 20 Jul USD 1,000 advance from Capital Builders, Abu Dhabi (entered as AED 3,672.50, the app is AED-only); 5 Aug final invoice 150 at 36 with the advance deducted; 12 Aug zero-rated export of 50 at 35 to Oman; 25 Aug Dubai paid in full; 28 Aug credit note for 20 bags restocked; 29 Aug Ras vendor credit 10 at 22; 1 Sep Abu Dhabi paid 1,000; 10 Sep both suppliers paid; 15 Sep DEWA 1,200 + 5% (unpaid); 18 Sep entertainment lunch 400 + 20 VAT (blocked); 22 Sep refund of the Dubai credit balance 698.25; September locked; 1 Oct invoice of 10 bags to test the cut-off.

I wrote my own ledger before looking at what the product posted. The working is in the scratchpad, `v1/ledger.md`.

## Findings, most severe first

### 1. Money/VAT: refunding a customer's credit balance issues a second tax credit note

**Steps.**
1. Credit INV-2026-00001 for 20 bags with CN-2026-00001 (698.25) after the invoice was paid in full.
2. The customer now holds a 698.25 credit balance inside 1040.
3. Contacts > "Customer balance" shows 0, because it only reads 2050 overpayments. The credit-note list has no refund action.
4. The only refund path is Invoices > INV-2026-00001 > View payments > "Refund payment". Its dialog says the invoice "becomes due again".
5. Enter 698.25 dated 22 Sep.

**What the product posted.** It issued a new tax credit note, **CN-2026-00002**: Dr 4010 665, Dr 2020 33.25, Cr 1040 698.25. It also posted the refund: Dr 1040 / Cr 1020 698.25.

**Effect.**
- Sales and output VAT are reduced twice. Box 14 becomes -0.50 instead of +32.75 payable.
- Emirates Towers stays at -698.25 in 1040, yet is absent from the ageing.

**Can it be undone?** Voiding CN-2026-00002 is refused: "This credit note has refunds paid against it. Void the refunds first". That message also appears in English inside the Arabic screen. There is no screen to void the refund.

**Verdict.** The product is wrong. A refund of an existing credit is a cash movement, not a new supply adjustment.

### 2. Money/VAT: box 1 emirate split ignores the customer's emirate

The VAT audit sales report lists every supply as emirate "sharjah": the Dubai sale, the Abu Dhabi sale and advance, the credit notes, and even the Oman export. Autopilot therefore shows a single standard-rated figure, 17,570 / 878.50.

There is no place-of-supply or emirate field on the invoice or on the contact. Contacts only have free-text city and country.

For goods delivered to Dubai and Abu Dhabi customers, I expect 1b Dubai 12,835 / 641.75 and 1a Abu Dhabi 5,400 / 270. **Verdict: the product is wrong.** The return would misreport the emirate distribution on every quarter for any trader selling outside Sharjah.

### 3. Money: opening stock is posted on the day you post it, not on the opening date

The opening-balances screen has an opening date of 2026-07-01 and an "Opening stock" grid (200 x 18). It produced two entries:
- **JE-20260701-001** (1 Jul) contains **Dr 3040 Opening Balance Equity 3,600** as the balancing line.
- **JE-20261002-001**, dated **2 Oct 2026** (today), posts Dr 1070 / Cr 3040 3,600.

As a result, the balance sheet at 30 Sep shows Inventory 4,664 (should be 8,264) and Opening Balance Equity -3,600.

The opening stock movement is not listed on Inventory > Movements, although the item shows 200 on hand. The screen's own message, "Balancing amount to Opening Balance Equity: AED 3,600 (debit)", also misleads, because the stock grid is left out of its totals.

**Verdict: the product is wrong.** The opening stock entry must carry the opening date.

### 4. Money: a vendor credit cannot return stock, so 1070 and the stock card drift apart

The vendor credit lines have no product or quantity field. I posted the 10-bag return to account 1070. The GL is right (Cr 1070 220, Cr 1050 11, Dr 2010 231), but:
- the item still shows 420 bags at an average of 20.20;
- the Inventory Summary shows 8,484 against the GL;
- every later sale is costed at 20.20 instead of 20.156: the 1 Oct sale posted 202.00 where I expect 201.56.

This contradicts the release-notes claim that "inventory always equals stock x average cost". **The product is wrong.**

### 5. Data integrity: stock movements dated on status change, not document date

Sales movements for INV-1, INV-3 and INV-4 (July and August) are dated **2 Oct 2026**, the day I changed each from Draft to Sent. The credit note restock is dated 2 Oct instead of 28 Aug.

The GL cost-of-goods-sold entries carry the correct invoice dates. Only the stock card does not, so a stock card by date is unusable for the auditor.

### 6. Compliance dead end: closing the month blocks the VAT return

I locked September from Month-End Close. Locking was allowed with the checklist at 6/7, with "No VAT return prepared" still open.

After that, three things fail with "Cannot post to locked period (09/2026). Unlock the period first." (in English inside the Arabic screen):
- VAT Filing > "Create official draft" for 1 Jul - 30 Sep;
- "Compute return";
- the VAT workpaper "Create / open".

The month-end page has no reopen or unlock control. The command palette has none, and the help article "Month-end and year-end close" does not mention one.

VAT Autopilot still computes the boxes, but not the official return, so the quarter's return could not be produced from the screens. **The product is wrong.** Either drafting a return must not count as posting, or locking must be refused until the return exists, or a reopen must be offered.

### 7. Compliance

- **Wrong period on VAT Filing.** The page's "current filing period" is 1 Oct - 31 Dec with deadline 28 Jan 2027. The Q3 return due on 28 Oct is not offered.
- **Spurious overdue returns.** Autopilot lists eight quarters back to Q4 2024 as overdue (متأخرة) for a company whose books start on 1 Jul 2026.
- **False Autopilot mismatch.** Autopilot warns "output VAT differs from the ledger by -AED 17.50". That 17.50 is the VAT on the 1 Oct invoice: the ledger tie leaks across the boundary, even though the return itself is right.
- **Reason missing from the credit note PDF.** The reason entered on CN-2026-00001 ("returned, damaged packaging") is not printed. A tax credit note should state why it was issued.
- **Wrong box in the help.** The VAT help says blocked VAT is "excluded from box 10". It should say box 9.
- **USD advance not supported.** Advances are AED only: no currency, no rate, and no exchange gain or loss on settlement. The USD advance had to be converted by hand.

### 8. Usability: a bookkeeper would post wrongly here

- **Bank account not linked.** The bank account created during onboarding is saved as "Not linked" to any ledger account. As a result, Bill Pay > Record Payment does **not offer 1020 Bank** and **defaults to 1030 Petty Cash**. The fix is hidden under Bank Reconciliation > "Bank accounts" tab > Edit > Ledger account.
- **Expense amount field.** "Amount" is the net amount (420 + VAT 20 previewed as 440) and the label does not say so. Editing an expense blanks its date; the date shows as raw `2026-09-18T00:00:00.000Z`.
- **Native confirm on vendor credit.** Approval uses a native browser confirm, which does nothing in some embedded views.
- **Invoice numbering.** The number is prefilled with a timestamp ("INV-1790954137194"); a typed number was silently replaced.
- **Row menus.** The status dropdown still offers Paid. Credit notes offer Add Payment, Set Recurring and Delete. Approved bills offer Edit and Delete.
- **Contacts and onboarding.** Contacts have no emirate field and require an email. The sign-up TRN is not carried into onboarding. Onboarding never asks for filing frequency, financial year or inventory costing.
- **Stock figure.** The first sale showed "In stock: 10" with 1,000 bags on hand.

### 9. Arabic

Left in English inside the Arabic screens: server errors ("Cannot post to locked period…", "This credit note has refunds…"), "No VAT return prepared for this period", status "Issued", emirate "sharjah", all journal descriptions, "Meals & Entertainment", account names in the expense preview, "The financial year has not ended yet.", Autopilot "Draft"/"Quarterly". The rest (invoice dialog, calendars, account names) was correct.

### 10. Cosmetic (PDFs) and mobile

**PDFs.** The tax invoice, advance tax invoice and tax credit note PDFs are bilingual. They show supplier and customer TRN and address, and the credit note references the original invoice and its date. The opening-balance PDF correctly says "This is not a tax invoice". Defects:
- On the advance tax invoice, the "ADVANCE TAX INVOICE" title overlaps the company name.
- On the same PDF, the description runs out of its row, and the customer address lacks "Abu Dhabi".
- The tax invoice shows the line discount as "5%" with no amount.
- Paid and part-paid invoices still print "TOTAL DUE" for the full amount.

**Mobile.** At 375 px there was no horizontal overflow on Invoices, the New Invoice dialog, Bill Pay, Customer Advances, VAT Autopilot, Trial Balance, Month-End Close or Inventory.

## Could the quarter be done through the screens alone?

Almost. Every transaction was entered through the screens; I never used the API (which, for the record, has no credit notes, advances or VAT endpoints).

Three things failed:
- the official VAT 201 after the month-end lock (no reopen in the UI);
- a correct refund of a customer credit balance (the only path issues a second credit note);
- returning stock to a supplier (vendor credits have no item).

## Sign-off

**VAT return, Q3 2026: WOULD NOT SIGN.**
- Box 1 puts all supplies in Sharjah.
- Box 1 and box 14 are understated by 665 / 33.25 because of the duplicate credit note.
- The app cannot produce the official return once the month is closed.

**Year-end, on these books: WOULD NOT SIGN.**
- Inventory 1070 (4,664 at quarter-end; 8,062 today) disagrees with the stock card (420 bags / 8,484).
- 3040 carries a stray 3,600 at the quarter-end.
- Receivables in the GL (14,649.25) differ from the ageing (15,347.50).
- Net profit is understated by 665.

What did work:
- weighted-average cost of goods sold;
- advance VAT timing;
- blocked input VAT;
- discounts and shipping;
- the quarter cut-off;
- month-end with no closing entries;
- bill-to-inventory posting.

## Competitor remark

I would not move this client from Zoho Books, and only cautiously from QuickBooks. Zoho records an emirate (place of supply) on every invoice and splits box 1 by it. Zoho also refunds a credit note's balance directly, and its vendor credits carry items and quantities. For an inventory trader selling across emirates, those three things are the job.

Muhasib's VAT engine is closer than in earlier rounds; the blocked-input, advance and cut-off logic are right. Wafeq would still win this client today on the emirate split alone.

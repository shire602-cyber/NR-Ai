# Teardown 6 — t4: Desert Rose Trading LLC (banking, FX, fixed assets, close)

Tester: independent UAE accountant, black-box only (browser, help centre, reports, `/api/v1/openapi.json`). Port 5096, database `muhasib_p9_t4`.

## Scenario summary

Desert Rose Trading LLC, Abu Dhabi, VAT quarterly, FY = calendar year. FAB current account (AED), FAB USD account, FAB credit card. Opening position by journal (1 Jan 2025 and 31 Dec 2025), year-end close 2025, then Q3 2026 kept from bank statements:

- One statement file with 25 Q3 lines in five formats: CSV, OFX, MT940, CAMT.053 and a text-layer PDF, plus a USD CSV and an October CSV with an overlap line and two identical same-day lines.
- Six invoices: one receipt covers two invoices, one receipt overpays (9,000 against 8,400), and one zero-rated USD export is paid into the USD account.
- Five bills, including an AED 84,000 vehicle and an outstanding cheque.
- Bank rules: DEWA, Etisalat, salaries split 80/20, a bank service charge with VAT, a TT charge without VAT, and the card settlement.
- Bank loan with interest.
- Own-account transfer from AED to USD, then USD revaluation at quarter end.
- New Hilux (84,000, 4 years, 10% residual), depreciation catch-up to September, and disposal of a 2024 van at a gain.
- Month-end lock for July to September, then attempts to post into the locked quarter.
- About a third of the work was done in Arabic: October import, rules, matching and every review screen. Five screens were checked at 375 px.

Two things I had to do through the internal API, because the UI has no path for them (see F1):

- create the bank accounts and GL accounts;
- set "reconcile from".

Two environment notes, not product findings:

- The shared browser's `localhost` cookies collided with the other testers' sessions, so I browsed through a port-isolating proxy.
- My own automation dated three Q3 journals a month early (still inside Q3), and dated two receipts 2 Oct instead of 30 Sep. Quarter totals below account for this.

## Findings, most severe first

### Money and VAT wrong

1. **The VAT 201 form computes boxes 12–14 without the adjustment column.** Reproduce: post a manual journal that touches 2020 or 1050 inside the period (here the van-sale VAT of 2,000 and two card receipts with 25 and 15 VAT), then generate the Q3 draft and open Edit. Boxes 12, 13 and 14 show 2,950.00, 7,527.50 and (4,577.50) "Refundable". The stored record, the history row and the VAT control report all say 4,950.00, 7,567.50 and (2,617.50). Anyone keying EmaraTax from this screen over-claims **1,960.00**. The stored figure is right and the screen is wrong.
2. **Journal-based VAT reaches the boxes with no net amount.** The VAT on the asset disposal and on the credit-card purchases lands only in the adjustment column. Box 1a understates supplies by 40,000.00 and box 9 understates expenses by 800.00. The disposal screen has no VAT field, and the card can't be used as a payment account (F11), so a journal was the only route.
3. **Bill payments have no bank-account picker.** Reproduce: Bill Pay → AFL-0920 → Record Payment, which offers only amount, date, method and reference. The cheque of 2,100 was posted Cr **1020 Bank Accounts**, not FAB 1021. So it never shows as an outstanding cheque in the FAB reconciliation, and the balance sheet shows "Bank Accounts" as a 2,100 liability. Supplier payments matched from the bank screen did post to 1021 correctly.
4. **Depreciation catch-up writes into old years and skips closed ones.** Reproduce: add an asset bought 1 Apr 2024 (there is no opening accumulated depreciation field), then run depreciation for January 2026. The run silently posted nine journals dated Apr–Dec **2024**, with no backdating confirmation (manual journals do ask). It skipped all twelve months of the closed FY2025, then posted January 2026. The ledger double-counted 9,000 against my opening balances. The register shows 2024 as "Posted", then a gap, then 2026.
5. **The asset can't be disposed of, and the year can't be reopened.** Dispose FA-001 on 31 Aug 2026 → "Cannot post to locked period (01/2025)". The register tries to back-fill the closed year. Year-end "Reopen" → HTTP 403 "Only a firm owner can reopen a closed financial year". The UI showed a stale toast instead of that message. So a company owner can never dispose of this asset. The register stays at 144,000 against 84,000 in the ledger, and the asset depreciates again every month: September posted another 1,000, which I reversed by hand.
6. **No foreign-currency invoices, despite the help article.** The help article "Exchange rates" says "Choose the currency on an invoice or bill". The invoice form, new or edit, has no currency field; bills do. I raised the USD 12,000 export as AED 44,070. The receipt at 3.67 left 30.00 that can only be written off by journal, so AR ageing (6,330) no longer ties to 1040 (6,300), which contradicts the release-note promise. There is also no bank-balance revaluation: FX Gains & Losses covers invoices and bills only, so the 97.51 unrealised gain was a manual journal.
7. **Foreign-currency bank reconciliation doesn't work.** The ledger side shows -USD 25.00. It ignores the USD 10,000 opening balance, the transfer, the Atlas receipt and the revaluation, leaving a difference of USD 10,000.

### Compliance and audit trail

8. **The AED reconciliation reaches zero by listing false items.** Its job is to explain every reconciling item, and it explains things that aren't reconciling items:
   - The matcher never offers a combination, so one receipt for two invoices (31,500 = 21,000 + 10,500) can't be matched.
   - An overpayment can't be matched either. Recording the payment says "Payment 9000.00 exceeds the outstanding balance… Re-send with allowCredit=true", a raw API message with no button behind it.
   - The second leg of a cross-currency own-account transfer gets no suggestion.
   - "Create entry" allows a single account only: AR is excluded, and a loan instalment can't be split into principal and interest.
   - A reviewer would read six phantom items as real. Before I set "reconcile from" (API only), the 2024 and 2025 opening journals appeared as deposits in transit and the difference was 295,275.
9. **The bank match dialog keeps the last account chosen.** When my pick failed, "Create entry and match" posted the Gulf Horizon receipt of 9,000 to **2210 Loan Payable**, the account from the previous line. Unmatch reversed it cleanly. The "This line is matched" panel doesn't say what the line was matched to.
10. **An unclosed prior year isn't rolled forward.** The FY2024 depreciation of 9,000 appears in the 30 Sep 2026 trial balance under 5100, and in balance-sheet "current-year earnings".
11. **Dates are a day early.** Entry numbers (JE-20241231-001 for 1 Jan 2025), the reconciliation report ("9 Aug" for a 10 Aug journal), the VAT adjustment list and the suggestions page all show the previous day.

### Data entry blockers and data loss risk

12. **Bank accounts and GL accounts can't be created in the UI.**
    - The import dialog says "Create one first under the bank account settings", but no such page exists and `/bank-accounts` returns 404.
    - Accounts → Add Account fails with "Validation error", because a code is required and there is no code field.
    - Chart of Accounts → Add Account navigates to Journal.
    - The bank list is limited to Emirates NBD, ADCB, FAB, Mashreq and Other.
    - A credit card can't be a bank account (the linked account must be an asset), and an expense can't be paid from a liability.
13. A customer payment can't be deleted or voided: Payment History has only Close.
14. The journal list shows only the latest 25 entries, with no pagination or notice.
15. The Add Contact, rule and match dialogs stay open with the previous values after saving. A customer inherited another customer's TRN, a contact became a Vendor, and three rules failed silently at "Total 120%" under a stale "Rule created" toast. Duplicate TRNs are accepted, and a due date before the bill date is accepted (the bill immediately goes "Overdue").

### Cosmetic

## What works, and works well

- **Duplicate detection across formats is excellent.** CSV gave 25 new lines. OFX, MT940, CAMT.053 and the PDF each reported "0 new, 25 already existed". The PDF review grid proves opening balance + lines = closing balance. In October the overlap line was skipped and two genuine identical same-day lines were both kept.
- **Rules preview exact Dr/Cr lines:** DEWA 1,200 + 60, salaries 36,000 / 9,000, service charge 100 + 5, TT charge 75 with no VAT. All 13 posted in one action, and their VAT reached box 9.
- **The lock held five different ways:**
  - a manual journal dated 15 Sep;
  - a new invoice dated 20 Sep;
  - moving an August invoice's date;
  - unmatching a September bank line;
  - "create entry" on a September bank line.

  Each was refused with "Cannot post to locked period (MM/2026)".
- **The bank-feeds screens are honest.** The feeds panel is hidden when no provider is configured. The import tab says live feeds aren't required, and the Bank Feed Sync Status report says "No bank feed is connected. Statements are imported manually."
- **The arithmetic is right:** daily pro-rata depreciation, USD fee at the dated rate (91.88), P&L to the fils, VAT control totals, journal backdating confirmation, idempotent depreciation re-run.

## Verdicts

**VAT return: WOULD NOT SIGN as produced.**

- The net refund of 2,617.50 is correct.
- Box 1a understates standard-rated supplies by 40,000.
- Box 9 understates expenses by 800.
- The return screen a preparer would copy from shows a refund of 4,577.50.

I would sign only after overriding boxes 1a and 9 by hand and filing from the history totals, not from the form.

**Year-end and quarter close: WOULD NOT SIGN.**

- The fixed-asset register disagrees with 1240/1290 by 60,000 / 18,000.
- The van can't be disposed of in the module.
- The USD account doesn't reconcile.
- The "balanced" AED reconciliation rests on six phantom items.
- AR ageing doesn't tie to 1040.
- The unclosed FY2024 result is presented as current-year earnings.
- Reopening 2025 is impossible for the company owner.

## Competitor remark

I would not move this client from Zoho Books, QuickBooks or Wafeq yet, for three reasons:

- **Those products already do what fails here.** All three let you set up bank and card accounts on screen, raise foreign-currency invoices with realised and unrealised FX, apply one receipt across several invoices (or keep the excess as a credit), split a bank line, and revalue a foreign-currency bank balance.
- **Muhasib is ahead in some places.** Its import (five formats, real duplicate detection, a PDF balance proof), rule previews and lock enforcement beat Wafeq and match Zoho.
- **The core accountant workflows don't hold.** Multi-currency, asset disposal, the reconciliation explanation and the VAT form's boxes 12–14 aren't trustworthy for a client like Desert Rose. Fix F1–F8 and F12 and the answer changes.

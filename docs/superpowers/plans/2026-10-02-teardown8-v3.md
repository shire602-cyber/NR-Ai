## New findings

**N1. HIGH: "Opening leave days" is copied into every leave type.**
- **Steps:** enter Opening leave days 15 (Maria: 25) on the employee form, then open Leave › Balances.
- **What happens:** every type opens at 15 or 25 with zero accrual:
  - Sick shows 15 instead of 90;
  - Maternity shows 15 instead of 60;
  - Hajj shows 15 instead of 30;
  - Bereavement and Parental show 15 instead of 5;
  - Study shows 15 instead of 10.
- **The employee sees it too:** Maria's home page shows sick, maternity and hajj each at 25 days.
- **Sick leave history:** Sunil's 15 June sick days, taken before the as-of date, no longer count as taken (Taken 5, balance 10).
- **What still works:** the September tier-2 deduction was right (1,000).
- **Effect:** statutory entitlements are misstated to staff and HR. The field should apply to annual leave only.

**N2. MEDIUM: editing an employee moves dates back one day.**
- **Steps:** open Edit on an unchanged employee. The form shows the as-of date as 2026-06-29, although I entered 30 Jun.
- **What happens on save:** Maria's as-of date became 28 Jun and her join date became 31 Mar 2023. Each later save moves them back another day.
- **Effect:** a moved join date changes gratuity service. I re-entered both dates before the settlement.
- **Verdict:** a silent date regression of the kind the Phase 9 date fix was meant to end.

**N3. LOW–MEDIUM: the payslip pay date is the recording date, not the payment date.**
- Payments were recorded with dates 31 Jul, 31 Aug and 30 Sep.
- Every payslip prints "Pay Date: 2 Oct 2026" (the day I recorded them).

**N4. LOW–MEDIUM: the catch-up journal stops prior service at the first payroll period.**
- It is labelled correctly and dated the run (31 Oct), Dr 3020.
- For the 2020 joiner it computed 6.5 years (25,000 gratuity, 15 leave days), which is service to 30 Jun.
- His service to 30 Sep is 6.75 years (26,267). The 3 months (about 1,267 gratuity and 7.5 leave days) fall into neither the catch-up nor any run.
- This only affects employees added after the first payroll run with an old join date.

**N5. LOW: the employee form fails with only "Validation error".**
- It happens when Employee number is empty.
- The message is in English and does not name the field.

My test artifacts outside Q3, all in October: the test employee "ZZ Test NoOpening", an unapproved October run, and the catch-up journal JE-20261031-001. None of them touches the 30 Sep figures.

# Teardown 6, t5: accounting-firm partner and external auditor (2026-10-02)

## What I tested

I acted as a partner at a Dubai firm deciding whether to move 40 clients onto Muhasib, and as the external auditor of those clients. I set up three companies, all with a January to December financial year:

- **Noor Dubai Trading LLC** (Dubai). Turnover is small, so Small Business Relief (SBR) should apply.
- **Falcon Industrial Supplies LLC** (Abu Dhabi). Revenue of AED 3.2m, so SBR does not apply.
- **Zoho Migrated Trading LLC**, built from a Zoho-style export through the migration wizard.

On Noor and Falcon I set an approval rule on every bill and every manual journal: a staff accountant enters, the partner (Owner) approves. The staff accountant worked in English on Noor and in Arabic on Falcon. Q3 2026 activity was share capital, standard-rated sales, a zero-rated export, rent and fees, salaries, a client-entertainment dinner, receipts and payments. I approved some items and rejected one bill.

I then ran the VAT 201, FTA audit file (FAF), trial balance, P&L, balance sheet and ageing; the corporate tax (CT) calculator and its Excel workpaper; the "Management roll-up" (the product's consolidation); API v1 with two keys; a ZIP export of Noor; deletion of Falcon; two-factor login (2FA) and "require 2FA"; the audit trail; and a September lock. I checked five screens at 375 px in Arabic, and wrote my expected entries into a scratch ledger before reading any output.

**Where I could not use the screens:**
- There is no screen to create a second or third company. I used `POST /api/companies` from the logged-in session.
- I used the session API to read account IDs, because API v1 has no accounts endpoint (see F13).

**Test environment:** the browser pane was shared with other testers. Cookies on `localhost` are shared across ports, so another tester's login overwrote my session ("Invalid token"). I restarted once with `t5.localhost` (partner) and `t5s.localhost` (staff) as allowed origins. No data was lost.

## Findings, most severe first

### Money and VAT wrong

**F1. Critical. Any document dated the 1st of a month is reported in the previous month by the VAT return, VAT summary and FAF.**
- *Repro:* as partner in Noor, create an invoice dated **1 Oct 2026** for 10,000 + 500 VAT and mark it Sent. Regenerate the VAT draft for 1 Jul – 30 Sep.
- *Result:*
  - The VAT draft shows output VAT 5,000 and payable **3,000** (correct is 2,500).
  - The VAT summary report for Q3 shows 120,000 / 5,000.
  - The Q3 FAF lists the invoice dated 2026-09-30. The Q4 FAF is empty.
  - The P&L and trial balance for Q3 correctly leave it out, so the return no longer ties to the ledger.
- *Same cause:* every invoice and journal date in the FAF is one day early (5 Jul shows as 2026-07-04), and JV-A1 (200,000) and JV-B1 (1,000,000), dated 1 July, are **missing from the Q3 FAF GL**. Entry numbers carry the previous day (JE-20260630-001 for 1 July). The export writes naive timestamps (`2026-06-30 20:00:00`), so an auditor puts 1 July journals in June. Bill, payment and API-created dates are stored at 00:00 and not shifted.
- *Verdict:* the product is wrong. This puts sales in the wrong tax period and FAF records on the wrong dates, and would draw FTA penalties.

**F2. High. Input VAT on client entertainment is recovered in full.**
- *Repro:* in Falcon, enter a bill for a client hospitality dinner: 20,000 + 1,000 VAT.
- *Result:* box 9 shows 1,520,000 / 76,000 and box 14 shows 84,000. The bill form only takes a free VAT-rate number; there is no "blocked / non-recoverable" option.
- *Verdict:* this breaches Art. 53 of the Executive Regulation. The correct payable is 85,000. The FAF repeats the error with tax code SR.

**F3. High. The corporate tax screens ignore SBR and give no way to add back disallowed expenses.**
- There is no way to elect SBR. For eligible Noor, the workpaper says "Small business relief: Not applied".
- The computation sheet labels AED 375,000 as the "Small-business relief threshold". That figure is the 0% band; the SBR threshold is AED 3m of revenue (MD 73/2023).
- The only adjustment field is "Deductions". The help page says to "enter adjustments, such as non-deductible expenses", but nothing allows an add-back. Falcon therefore comes out at 90,450 instead of 91,350.
- Profit before tax is labelled "Gross Profit".
- The "Generated" time on the workpaper is UTC.

### Controls an auditor relies on

**F4. Critical. Rejecting a document does not take it out of the approval queue, and it can then be posted.**
- *Repro:*
  1. Staff enters bill DPS-552 (8,400). The partner opens Approvals, clicks Reject and gives a reason.
  2. The toast says "Document rejected". The History pane shows "Rejected by Layla Partner" with the reason.
  3. The queue (filter: Waiting) still shows DPS-552 as "Pending approval" with Approve and Reject buttons. The bill status stays "pending".
  4. Click Approve. Result: "The document was approved and posted". The bill posted to 25 Sep: expense 8,000 and input VAT 400 in Q3, payables at 30 Sep now 18,900. The staff member never resubmitted it.
- The same happened with a rejected journal (JE-20261002-003): still pending after rejection.
- The staff member's bill list shows "pending", with no rejection notice and no reason.
- *Verdict:* the reject decision has no effect. An auditor cannot rely on the approval workflow.

**F5. High. The audit trail misses key events.**
- The *Audit Trail* report does show user, IP address and details. The *Activity History* page has no user column.
- Never recorded: bill creation and **bill edits** (staff changed the dates of three pending bills without trace); contacts; team invitations and roles; company creation and settings; CT drafts; refused actions (staff "Post"/"Approve" 403s); and VAT draft generation. A regenerated draft **silently overwrote** the earlier 2,500 draft, and the export keeps only the latest.
- The Journal Report's "Created by" column shows the approver (Layla Partner) for bill journals, not the person who prepared them.
- Voids are logged as `{"status":"void"}`, with no reason and no previous status.

**F6. Medium. FAF content gaps.**
- The export to Muscat Traders (Oman) has Country = **AE**.
- There is no opening-balance section, so the running balances (for example, bank opening at 252,500) cannot be reconciled from the file.
- The GL totals are short, as described in F1.

**F7. Medium. Locks and voids.**
- *What works:* the September lock refused a new invoice dated 20 Sep.
- *What does not:* the accountant could **void** a 5 Sep tax invoice with no confirmation, reason or tax credit note (the reversal was dated 2 Oct, so the locked month was untouched, but an issued tax invoice should be corrected by credit note). The lock record shows "01:36 PM" (UTC; Dubai was 17:36). The month-end checklist says "No VAT return prepared" although a Q3 draft existed.

**F8. Medium. Records retention is stated as "5 years, as UAE law requires".** Corporate tax records must be kept for 7 years (Federal Decree-Law 47/2022, Art. 56). The deletion page, and the "cannot be deleted before 2031-10-02" message, both use 5 years.

### Data deletion and export

**F9. High. Deleting a company leaves the next screen pointing at a different company.**
- *Repro:* after deleting Falcon (which needs password, 2FA code and typed name; good), Data & privacy shows "Falcon … will be anonymised on November 1, 2026 – Restore company".
- On the same screen "Request export" exported **Noor** (`POST /companies/<Noor>/exports`) and "Delete company…" would act on Noor. *Risk:* the wrong client gets deleted.
- No screen exports the deleted company, though the server accepts it. The owner cannot read the books said to be "kept read-only".
- The **accountant** can request and download full-company ZIPs, including the owner's export of the deleted company (HTTP 200, 55 KB). Only "Delete" is owner-only.

### Firm workflow

**F10. High. There is no product for an accounting firm.**
- `/api/firm/*` answers "NRA firm staff access required". An outside firm cannot have a firm workspace, a cross-client approval queue or client health views.
- There is no screen to add a company. The company switcher only appears once a second company exists.
- After switching, the accountant was sent to `/onboarding` for the client company, prefilled with **the other company's name** (Noor while Falcon was active). One click would rename the client.
- Approvals: the queue is per company, does not show who prepared each item, and approves in one click with no comment. With one partner, a bill the partner enters can never be approved ("Nobody else can give approval step 1").
- Consolidation is one summary table with no eliminations. "Open statements" opens a single company, headed "31 Dec 2025 – 3 Oct 2026".
- Invitations say "Invitation sent" while email is off. The invitee becomes a passwordless account named after their email, reachable only through a password-reset link.

**F11. Medium. Two-factor login.**
- *What works:* staff sessions were revoked, staff API keys answered 403 `TWO_FACTOR_REQUIRED`, and the enrolment screen with recovery codes is good, in Arabic too.
- *What does not:*
  - Turning "Require 2FA" on logged the owner out immediately, with no warning.
  - Noor's setting blocked the staff member from **every** client, Falcon included, until they enrolled.

### API v1

**F12. What works:** the OpenAPI document matches the docs page. Idempotency is right: a missing key gives 400, a replay returns `Idempotent-Replayed: true`, and reusing a key with a new body gives 422. A missing scope gives 403 `SCOPE_MISSING`, the 11th call in a minute gives 429 with `Retry-After`, and approval rules hold through the API (403 `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`). VAT on 3 × 333.33 is correctly 50.00.

**F13. Medium. API defects.**
- There is no accounts endpoint, but payments require `paymentAccountId` and journals require `accountId` as UUIDs. An integrator cannot find them.
- `paymentTerms: net30` was ignored (`dueDate: null`).
- The overpayment error says "re-send with allowCredit=true", but the schema rejects unknown properties (`additionalProperties: false`).
- Error details give money as numbers, although the API promises strings.
- The accountant can see the owner's key and has a "Revoke" button for it.

### Arabic and usability

**F14. Arabic.** Date pickers and calendars are English ("October 2nd, 2026", "Su Mo"). Account names in journals and the bill account list are English, without codes. Saving a draft journal says "تم ترحيل قيد اليومية" ("posted"), and "إجمالي المدين مدين" repeats a word. Server errors, the statuses "partial", "Draft" and "paid", and months on the bill list and VAT page are English. At 375 px the journal memo wraps one word per line; invoices, bills, VAT and approvals had no horizontal scroll.

**F15. Usability.**
- Bills accept a due date before the bill date, and a future bill date.
- The approval-rule amount box is prefilled with 5000 (typing "0" gave 50,000).
- The VAT page's "current filing period" shows Q4 while Q3 is due and unfiled.
- Company profile offers "Annually" filing and a "Flat Rate" registration, which do not exist in the UAE.
- Invoice status can be set to "Paid" without recording a payment.
- Migration wizard: a missing go-live date fails silently. With no open-items file chosen it said "Ready to post" although receivables of 31,500 had no open invoices behind them.

## Would I sign?

**VAT returns: WOULD NOT SIGN.**
- Falcon: the product says 84,000; correct is 85,000 (F2).
- Noor: the regenerated return says 3,000; correct is 2,500 (F1).
- The FAF dates every invoice one day early and leaves out 1 July journals.

**Year-end and CT: WOULD NOT SIGN.**
- There is no SBR election or record for Noor (F3).
- Falcon's CT is understated by 900 because nothing can be added back (F3).
- As auditor, I cannot rely on the controls: a rejected bill was posted (F4), and bill edits leave no trace (F5).

## Competitor view

For one well-kept SME the ledger is sound: trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, ageing, export and Zoho migration all tied to the dirham, and API v1 is better built than Wafeq's. I would still not move a client from Zoho Books or QuickBooks: Zoho assigns VAT periods by local date, while Muhasib moves anything dated the 1st into the previous quarter; QuickBooks Online Accountant and Zoho's partner portal give a firm one client list and one approval inbox, while here there is no firm workspace or way to add a client; and Wafeq at least records the SBR election.

Fix F1, F2, F3, F4 and F5, then ask me again.

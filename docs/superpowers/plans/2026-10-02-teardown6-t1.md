# Teardown 6 – t1: Al Qasimi Building Materials LLC (inventory, advances, Q3 VAT 201)

Independent UAE accountant review, 2 Oct 2026. Port 5093, database `muhasib_p9_t1`. Judged only through the browser UI, the help centre, the produced PDFs/report data, `/api/v1/openapi.json`, and the two permitted docs.

**Environment note.** The shared browser pane shares `localhost` cookies across ports, so other agents' logins overwrote mine. Before any transaction I restarted my server with origin `http://t1.localhost:5093` and used a background tab. The product itself is unchanged.

## Scenario summary

- **Company.** Sharjah LLC with a single establishment, VAT-registered, filing quarterly, financial year = calendar year. It went live on 1 Jul 2026 with opening balances dated 30 Jun:
  - bank 50,000
  - AR 12,600 (Al Noor, Sharjah)
  - AP 8,400 (Emirates Cement)
  - stock 200 bags at 18 = 3,600
  - share capital 57,800
- **Item.** One tracked item: CEM-50, weighted average cost.
- **Q3 transactions:**
  - Bills: 500 bags at 20 (5 Jul), 300 bags at 22 (20 Jul).
  - Gulf Towers (Dubai), 1 Aug: 400 bags at 35, 5% line discount, AED 200 shipping.
  - Capital Builders (Abu Dhabi): advance of USD 1,000, entered as AED 3,672.50 because advances are AED only, on 10 Aug. Final invoice on 25 Aug: 150 bags at 36, with the advance applied.
  - Muscat Trading (Oman): zero-rated export, 50 bags at 34, on 1 Sep.
  - Gulf Towers paid in full (5 Sep). Credit note for 20 returned bags, restocked (10 Sep). Credit balance refunded (20 Sep).
  - Union Cement vendor credit for 10 bags at 22 (15 Sep), applied to its bill.
  - Capital Builders paid 1,000 of 1,997.50 (15 Sep).
  - Both cement bills paid (25 Sep).
  - Client entertainment lunch 500 + 25 VAT, VAT blocked (18 Sep).
  - DEWA bill 1,000 + 50 VAT (28 Sep), paid on 30 Sep.
- **Close.** September closed and locked; Q3 VAT 201 draft generated.
- **Arabic.** About a third of the work was done in Arabic: the advance, the final invoice, the export, receipts, the credit note attempts, the vendor credit and the refund.

I wrote my ledger (`scratchpad/t1/ledger.md`) before reading any product output.

## Findings (ordered by severity)

### Money / VAT wrong

**F1 – VAT and journal dates are stored in UTC, so a document dated on the 1st falls into the previous month or quarter.**
- Repro: create any invoice with the date picker (browser in Dubai, +04).
  - INV-2026-00001 dated 1 Aug is stored as `2026-07-31T20:00Z` and posted as `JE-20260731-002`.
  - Reports → VAT Summary 1–31 Jul shows 13,500 / 675 for that invoice; 1–20 Aug excludes it.
  - The 1 Sep export shows in August.
- The P&L and Revenue-by-customer reports use the local date, so the reports disagree with each other.
- In this quarter no document fell on a boundary, so box totals are unaffected. However, any sale dated 1 Oct will be declared in Q3, and any sale dated 1 Jul in Q2.
- Verdict: product wrong. This is a filing-period error waiting to happen.

**F2 – Blocked input VAT on entertainment is recovered.**
- Repro: Purchases → Expenses → Add Expense Manually: amount 500, VAT 25, category "Entertainment" → Post.
- The post dialog preview shows "Dr Travel & Meals 525 / Cr Bank 525". The entry actually posted is Dr 5090 500, Dr 1050 25, Cr Bank 525 (JE-20260918-001). Box 9 then includes 500 / 25.
- There is no "VAT not recoverable" option and no entertainment account.
- Effect: overclaim of AED 25 (box 9 should be 17,380 / 869, payable 42.75 not 17.75). The preview misstates the posting.
- Verdict: product wrong (Article 53 of the VAT Executive Regulation).

**F3 – "Close September" destroys the year's P&L.**
- Repro: Month-End Close → September 2026 → Generate Closing Entries → Generate & Post → Lock Period.
- JE-20260930-002 closes the entire year-to-date P&L into 3020 Retained Earnings (6,719), dated 30 Sep. That figure even includes postings dated 2 Oct.
- After the close:
  - Q3 P&L shows revenue 0 and net profit −404.
  - The September P&L shows revenue −18,900.
  - A stray 404 debit balance on COGS remains at 30 Sep.
- Monthly closes should lock the period, not close revenue and expense to equity. The release notes themselves warn that folding profit into retained earnings wipes it from the P&L and the CT computation.
- Verdict: product wrong. High severity, because management accounts and the CT base are destroyed.

**F4 – Inventory costing is not usable end to end; the stock ledger diverges from the GL.**
1. **No UI switch for inventory costing.** The item form says "needs the company setting", but no settings page has the switch. I enabled it with `PATCH /api/companies/:id {inventoryCostingEnabled:true}`.
2. **Opening balances post 3,600 to 1070 but set item quantity to 0.** Loading the 200 bags through Add Stock → Adjustment posts another Dr 1070 / Cr 5210 3,600. I needed a reversing journal.
3. **Bills and purchase orders have no item field** (the public API bill line has no `productId` either), so purchases never add stock. Add Stock → Purchase posts Dr 1070 / Cr 2015 GRNI and tells you to code the bill to 2015. But 2015 is not offered in the bill's account list, so you get a double debit. I needed a 16,600 clearing journal.
4. **Stock movements have no date field.** Every movement is dated the day of entry (2 Oct). The 30 Sep inventory valuation therefore shows −600 bags / −12,120 against GL 7,860, and the restock of the returned 20 bags lands in October (Q3 COGS overstated by 404).
5. **Stock cannot be reduced in the UI.** Quantity has `min=1` and there is no negative adjustment. The purchase return went through `POST /api/products/:id/movements` with quantity −10. It posted at average cost (202), on top of the vendor credit's 220 credit to 1070, so another journal was needed.
6. **The unit-cost field pre-fills the static cost price (18)**, while the help text says movements are "always at average cost". A user who accepts the default on a purchase books it at 18.
- Verdict: product wrong. The release notes' claim that inventory ties to 1070 does not hold for any real purchase flow.

**F5 – A partial credit note is impossible in the UI.**
- The invoice row's "Create credit note" is a native `confirm()` that reverses the whole invoice.
- The Credit Notes → New credit note form fails on save with an English-only message: "Standalone credit-note writes are retired…".
- The help article claims "You can reduce part of the invoice".
- I issued CN-2026-00001 (−665 / −33.25) through `POST /api/companies/:id/invoices/:id/credit-note` with lines. There is also no restock option.
- Verdict: product wrong. Bookkeepers will either over-credit or give up.

### Compliance

**F6 – The credit note PDF is not a Tax Credit Note.** CN-2026-00001 prints:
- the title "TAX INVOICE";
- no reference to INV-2026-00001;
- "TOTAL DUE: AED −698.25", a due date and "Payment Terms: Net 30".

This is non-compliant with Article 60. Verdict: product wrong.

**F7 – Tax invoice content.**
- The recipient's address is never printed: there is no address field on the invoice, although the contact holds one. Article 59(1)(d) requires it for a registered recipient.
- The export invoice shows "VAT (0%)" with no zero-rating basis and no customer country.
- The opening-balance open item prints as a "TAX INVOICE" (OB-INV-1187), with "Opening balance … VAT 0%" and no customer TRN. Sent to a customer, it misstates a VAT-inclusive debt as a zero-rated supply.
- Verdict: product wrong.

**F8 – No refund path for a customer credit balance.**
- "Add payment" on the credit note is refused: "Payment amount must be a positive number", then "has nothing outstanding".
- Refunds exist only for advances. I used a manual journal (Dr 1040 / Cr Bank 698.25), which is not linked to the customer.

**F9 – Advances are AED only.** A USD advance cannot be recorded in USD. I converted it at 3.6725 by hand, and the advance invoice shows AED only.

**F10 – The VAT 201 page and PDF imitate the FTA return.** They carry "Federal Tax Authority | هيئة اتحادية", "www.tax.gov.ae", and "system generated, does not need to be signed". The Arabic legal name and the address print "-" / "N/A" even though the address is on file. A misleading look-alike; it should be a "worksheet".

**F11 – The month-end checklist passes things it should fail.**
- "Bank Reconciliation Complete – No bank transactions in this period" is shown although 1020 had nine September postings; there is no statement to reconcile.
- "VAT Return Prepared – none" is shown although a Q3 return covering September exists; quarterly filers are not understood.
- September can be locked while July and August stay open.

**F12 – Company setup.**
- Tax Registration Type offers "Flat Rate" and Filing Frequency offers "Annually"; neither exists in the UAE.
- The Company Profile page has no emirate, Arabic legal name or fiscal-year field. The help article says it does.
- The onboarding bank list offers Sharjah Islamic Bank, CBD, RAKBANK and others, but the server accepts only five values. The user sees a bare "Validation error".

### Data loss risk

**F13 – Approved bills still offer Edit and Delete**, and sent invoices still offer Edit. The Contacts page has a one-click "Clear All".

### Usability

- Unlabelled icon-only row actions on bills and vendor credits; journal numbers carry the UTC date (JE-20260919-001 for 20 Sep); raw ISO dates in the expenses list; "Journal entry posted" toast for a draft; no bank-account selector on bill payment; linking an invoice on the credit-note form does not fill the customer.
- 375 px: no horizontal overflow on invoices, VAT filing, trial balance, month-end, bill pay, customer advances or inventory.

### Arabic

- Date pickers and calendars stay in English ("October 2nd, 2026", Su/Mo).
- Statuses "paid" and "partial" are untranslated in toasts and lists.
- Account names in selects and journals are English.
- Server errors are in English.
- The stock dialog shows the English item name.
- The item VAT option "0% (معفى)" calls a zero rate exempt, which teaches users the wrong box (4 vs 5).
- Arabic in the PDFs renders correctly. The header "LLC" overlaps the TRN line.

## What worked

Boxes 1/4/8 and the emirate; discount and shipping; advance accounting (timing, netting, 2055 = 0); average-cost COGS at sale; export to 4060; vendor credit application; AR/AP sub-ledgers tie to the GL; the period lock; bilingual invoice PDFs with both TRNs.

## Sign-off

**VAT return: WOULD NOT SIGN.**
- Box 9 overclaims the 25 VAT on entertainment, so payable is 17.75 instead of 42.75.
- The credit note behind box 1 has no compliant credit-note document.
- The UTC date defect means I cannot trust period assignment for any future quarter without checking every document dated the 1st.

**Year-end: WOULD NOT SIGN.**
- The September close posted the year's result to retained earnings, leaving a −404 Q3 P&L.
- The inventory sub-ledger is negative at 30 Sep and cannot be dated.
- Purchases, credit notes and returns needed seven manual journals and three API calls to keep the GL right.

## Competitor remark

I would not move this client from Zoho Books (or QuickBooks/Wafeq) today:
- Zoho links items on bills and receives stock on the bill date.
- It issues partial credit notes with restock and refunds credit balances.
- It flags blocked input VAT.
- Its month-end lock does not touch the P&L.

Muhasib's VAT engine, advance handling and bilingual PDFs are genuinely good. But for a trading company with stock, the purchase-to-stock chain, credit notes, dated stock movements and the month-end close must work in the UI first.

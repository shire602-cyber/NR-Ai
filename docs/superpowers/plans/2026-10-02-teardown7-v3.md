# Teardown 7 / v3: payroll and HR verification after fixes (Gulf Horizon Facilities Management LLC)

Independent UAE accountant, no code access. I worked only in the running app at http://v3.localhost:5103 (own database `muhasib_p9_v3`), the help centre, and the files the app produced (SIF, payslip PDF). The public API v1 has no payroll endpoints. I used the session API only to download the same SIF and PDF files the buttons produce, and to probe Employee-role access. The server is now stopped.

## Findings (by severity)

**F1. CRITICAL: the Employee role can read the whole ledger and can create and post manual journals.**
- **Steps:**
  1. As owner, invite maria.v3 with the Employee role and link her login to her employee record.
  2. Log in as Maria.
  3. Journal Entries lists all 10 entries: capital, bank, opening EOSB, each payroll total, Maria's loan and her settlement. The "New entry" and "Reverse" buttons are shown.
  4. Chart of Accounts, Bank Reconciliation, Company Profile, Team (all emails) and Activity History (every login) also open for her.
  5. As Maria, the journal endpoint accepted a draft (Dr 5000 / Cr 1010, AED 1, dated 30 Sep). The post endpoint returned "Entry posted successfully". It is now JE-20260930-004 in the 30 Sep trial balance. I reversed it as owner; the reversal is JE-20261002-001, dated 2 Oct.
- **What is right:** reports correctly refuse her ("Reports are for accountants, CFOs and owners"). Payroll is correctly scoped to her own record, her own loan and her own settlement.
- **Verdict:** this breaks the help article ("An employee cannot open the company's invoices, bills, ledgers or reports") and any segregation of duties. A blocker.

**F2. HIGH: no payslip can be produced once a run is paid.**
- **Steps:**
  1. Open any paid run (Jul, Aug or Sep) and click the payslip icon.
  2. The toast says "Payslips are available once the payroll run has been calculated." The download endpoint returns PAYROLL_RUN_NOT_CALCULATED.
- **The only payslip I could get** was July's, at the "Calculated" stage before approval.
  - It has no DRAFT mark and shows "Pay Date: -".
  - Its content is correct: earnings, the employee's 5% GPSSA deduction, the employer 12.5% shown separately, a gratuity line, bilingual headings that render correctly.
- **The Arabic text layer is garbled** when extracted (e.g. "قيرة" for "فترة"), so search and copy fail. The visual rendering is fine.
- **The Employee role has no payslip tab at all.**
- **Effect:** I could not verify the unpaid, sick or loan lines on a payslip. Q3 has no issuable payslips.

**F3. HIGH (usability): "Record payment" and "Payroll register" are dead buttons inside the run detail view.**
- Clicking them does nothing.
- The payment dialog appears only after clicking "Back to payroll runs". The register button never opens anything; the register is only at Reports › Payroll register.
- A bookkeeper will conclude that payment cannot be recorded.

**F4. MEDIUM–HIGH (wrong period): the leave-pay provision books a year-to-date catch-up in the first run.**
- July 5029 is 34,666.67: Jan–Jul entitlement for five staff plus Maria's 10 carried days. August and September are 4,666.66 and 4,666.67.
- There is no opening leave-provision field and no warning before posting. July profit is understated by about 30,000.
- These days belong in the opening position (Dr retained earnings). The product posts them to the July P&L.

**F5. MEDIUM: the mid-month joiner is paid 16/30 for 17 days worked, with a 0.01 rounding error.**
- Ahmed joined on 15 Aug and worked 15–31 Aug (17 days). The product pays 5,000×16/30 + 1,066.67 + 266.67 = 4,000.01.
  - It underpays him 250 against 17/30, or 112.89 against 17/31.
  - It overpays 0.01 against its own 16/30 basis, through 5020, 2030, the bank and the SIF.
- His SIF row is internally inconsistent: start 2026-08-15, end 2026-08-31, but 16 days.
- **My verdict:** the product is wrong. Pay days worked, and round the total, not each component.

**F6. MEDIUM: the payroll register does not show its tie to the ledger.**
- It has no employer pension, gratuity or leave-provision columns.
- It does not split leave deductions (which reduce 5020) from pension and loan deductions (which are liabilities).
- It has no reconciliation line to 5020 and 2030, and the final settlement is not in it.
- The release notes' "register that ties to the ledger" is only true after manual work.

**F7. MEDIUM: a draft final settlement goes stale and is not under approval control.**
1. The draft was saved before September's payroll was approved. It showed loan 12,000 and net 9,200.
2. After the September instalment it still listed 9,200. The figures were recalculated only at posting (10,400 is correct).
3. There is no "final settlement" document type in Approval rules, so the accountant who prepared it can post it alone.

Also, Post uses a native browser confirm().

**F8. MEDIUM: unpaid leave still counts as service.**
- Sunil's 10 unpaid days did not reduce his gratuity accrual (about 155.56 over-accrued) or his annual-leave accrual.
- Article 51 excludes unpaid absence from service. The release notes already list this as known.

**F9. LOW–MEDIUM: payroll journal numbers carry the previous day's date.**
- The entry dated 31 Jul is numbered JE-20260730-001. The same happens on 31 Aug (JE-20260830) and 30 Sep (JE-20260929).
- The posting dates are correct, but the numbers still carry the old one-day-early date bug.

**F10. LOW: gaps in the audit trail.**
- The accountant's create, calculate and submit steps on payroll runs are not in the activity log. Only the owner's approve and payment steps are.
- The approvals queue "From" column is blank.

**F11. LOW: Arabic and cosmetic issues.**
- **English server text in the Arabic UI:**
  - the payslip error;
  - "Ahmed Hassan is pro-rated…" on the first calculation;
  - the GPSSA/gratuity note in the gratuity calculator;
  - "Reports are for accountants…".
- **Arabic labels and dates:**
  - the trial balance type column reads asset / liability;
  - the approval reference reads "Payroll 07/2026";
  - created dates read "Oct 02, 2026";
  - the gratuity calculator labels lose the year count ("( سنوات × 21 يومًا)").
- **Arabic names stored but not shown:** run tables show English names even though Arabic names are stored.
- **Grammar:** "needs approval from a owner".
- **Run table:** rows do not show the pension deduction in the Deductions column (Khalid's row shows 30,000 less 0 = 28,500).
- **Leave dialog:** it keeps the previous leave type when reopened.
- **Combined loan cap message:** a second 1,000 loan says "AED 1,000 is above 20 percent" instead of naming the combined 2,200.
- **Journal save:** saving a draft shows the toast "Journal entry posted".
- **Invitations:** they report "sent" with email not configured.
- **Employee form:** there is no contract type or contract end date, so the limited contract can only be recorded in free text.
- **Mobile:** at 375 px the payroll tabs (Employees, Runs, Leave, Loans, Settlement), Approvals and the Payroll register have no horizontal overflow. The tab strip scrolls in left-to-right order in RTL.

**Verified fixed and correct:**
- Gratuity accrual:
  - 21 days a year to 5 years, 30 days after;
  - UAE national excluded;
  - the settlement uses opening plus accrued provision and releases the difference.
- Pension 5% / 12.5% on the contract wage.
- Leave balances start at zero carry-forward (Maria's 10 days needed an explicit override, which is audit-logged).
- Unpaid and sick tier-2 deductions use the full wage over 30 days.
- The loan 20% cap is enforced across loans, and 1080 is cleared.
- The owner approval rule works and the preparer cannot approve.
- The SIF layout and IDs are correct, and the SIF is refused before approval.
- The trial balance and P&L tie exactly.

## Sign-off
- **VAT 201:** nil for this payroll-only quarter (no VAT accounts moved). I would sign a nil return.
- **Payroll and year-end: WOULD NOT SIGN.** The numbers are right, but:
  - an Employee-role user can post to the ledger (F1);
  - Q3 has no issuable payslips (F2);
  - the leave-pay provision puts about 30,000 of prior-period cost into July (F4);
  - the mid-month joiner is underpaid (F5).
- **When these are fixed:** the ledger mechanics are sign-off quality. Gratuity, pension, loans, the settlement, the SIF and the trial balance all reconciled to my independent figures.

## Competitor remark
The payroll ledger engine is now better than Zoho Books (no UAE gratuity, WPS or leave provision) and comparable to Wafeq. Settlement against the provision, the cross-loan 20% cap and a SIF tied to approval are real advantages.

I would not move a payroll client today. An employee who can post journals, plus a product that cannot reissue payslips after payment, would both fail a first audit-committee question. Fix F1–F3 and this becomes a reason to switch.

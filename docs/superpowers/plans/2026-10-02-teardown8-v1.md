## New findings

**N1 (VAT/money): voiding a credit note reverses it on the void date, not the credit note's date, and the return still counts it.**

Steps: issue CN-2026-00001 dated 28 Aug, then set its status to Void on 2 Oct. There is no date prompt; the release notes say "journal reversals take a date", but this path does not offer one.

Result:
- The reversal is posted as JE-20261002-001 (Dr 1040 698.25 / Cr 4010 665 / Cr 2020 33.25), dated **2 Oct**.
- The VAT audit sales report and the Q3 return still include the voided credit note at -665.00 / -33.25.

Effect on Q3:
- Dubai box 1: 12,170.00 / 608.50 (should be 12,835.00 / 641.75).
- Box 14: **-0.50 refundable** (should be **32.75 payable**).
- 1040 at 30 Sep: 14,649.25, while the ageing shows 15,347.50.

The year to date nets out (net result 6,797.00 = 6,649.00 + 148.00 for October), so the error is purely in the period. A voided tax credit note should be excluded from the return, or reversed on its own date.

**N2 (minor): the credit dialog still counts a voided credit note.** After the void, the credit dialog still showed "already credited AED 698.25" (creditable 13,476.75), and the quantity column still showed 400 creditable after 20 had been credited.

**N3 (compliance UX): the September checklist ignores a quarterly draft.** A Q3 draft covering September exists, yet September's checklist still says no VAT return is prepared and requires the lock override.

## Verdict

**Q3 VAT return: WOULD NOT SIGN** as these books stand. The return still counts the voided credit note (N1), giving box 14 -0.50 instead of 32.75 payable. The return mechanics I flagged before are fixed: the emirate split, the refund path, the period lock and its unlock, and the default period. N1 is the only remaining cause of the wrong figures.

**Year-end: WOULD NOT SIGN.** The stock card does not match 1070, because vendor credits still cannot move stock (420 bags against 410; Inventory Summary 8,484 against GL 8,264). The voided-credit-note dating (N1) also distorts period reporting.

Fixing N1 (exclude voided documents from the return, or reverse them on the document date) and adding a product line to vendor credits would, on this evidence, make both signable.

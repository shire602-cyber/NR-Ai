# Teardown 6 / t2: Nour Digital Consulting FZ-LLC (services, projects, Q3 2026)

Independent accountant's review, 2 October 2026. I used only the running app on port 5094, the help centre, `/api/v1/openapi.json`, the app's PDFs, and the two allowed docs.

## 1. Scenario and how it was run

Nour Digital Consulting FZ-LLC (Dubai, TRN 100234567800003, quarterly VAT, services only, capital 50,000 on 1 Jul). Al Waha (Dubai, P-0001, 400/h, 25% advance, partial invoice 31 Jul, final invoice 15 Sep with the advance deducted); Marina Bay (Dubai, P-0002, 350/h, quote 7,000 accepted online in Arabic and converted, invoice from unbilled 13 Aug, one disputed 8 h day); Saadiyat (Abu Dhabi, P-0003, 300/h, invoice 18 Aug, card payment with fee and a 1,000 partial refund, 2,000 monthly retainer with auto-send). Billable supplier costs on each project, a US software subscription under reverse charge (3,600), two employees on payroll Jul–Sep with WPS, late fees on, quarter-end statement. About 40% of the work was done in Arabic; six screens checked at 375 px.

Departures from the UI, disclosed: 15 of 17 time entries were posted to the app's own time-entry endpoint after the timer and manual entry were tested in the UI; the reverse-charge bill went through public API v1 (no UI path, F3); "Pay now" lands on an unreachable fake gateway, so the card payment, fee and refund were booked by hand (F4); recurring invoices and late fees run only from daily 06:00/06:30 UTC jobs with no "run now", so neither ran and both are excluded from the figures. Environment, not product: all agents share one cookie jar on `localhost`, so I ran on `t2.localhost`; the test browser auto-cancels native `confirm()` dialogs, which I overrode.

## 3. Findings, by severity

### Money and VAT wrong

**F1. A partial credit note is impossible, and the forced full credit lands in the wrong VAT period (High; product wrong).** Credit Notes > New Credit Note, pick INV-2026-00004, line "8 h x 350", save: **410 Gone** ("Standalone credit-note writes are retired"), yet the help article still documents this button and partial credits. "Create from invoice" is disabled. The invoice-row icon raises a native confirm ("reverse the journal entry") and creates CN-2026-00001 for the **full 9,660, dated today (2 Oct)**, with no date or amount choice; editing it fails silently (negative-quantity lines fail validation, no message); the time entries stay "billed". Effect: Q3 box 1b overstated by 9,200 / 460 (box 14 3,540 vs a correct 3,080); Q4 gets -9,200 and October's dashboard shows revenue of -9,200; the workaround (full credit plus replacement invoice) breaks ageing and late fees (F8).

**F2. Editing a draft invoice strips the project from every line (High; management reporting).** Create INV-2026-00006 from P-0001's unbilled work, Edit, "Apply advance", Save: all 8 lines return `projectId = null`. P-0001 shows 16,000 instead of 41,500; project revenue (34,800) no longer reconciles to the P&L (73,700), despite the release notes' "split by project on posting".

**F3. No reverse charge on purchases in the UI (High; compliance).** The bill form, bill edit and manual expense offer only a numeric "VAT%"; the internal edit call ignores a reverse-charge field; the VAT and bill help articles never mention reverse charge. Only public API v1 (`reverseCharge: true`, Professional plan) reaches boxes 3 and 10, and then the posting is exactly right (Dr 5080 3,600, Dr 1050 180, Cr 2020 180, Cr 2010 3,600). A bookkeeper will key 5% (180 input VAT with no output) or 0% (boxes 3 and 10 empty): both wrong returns.

**F4. Card payment, gateway fee and refund cannot be booked through the product (High).** Portal link > Pay now lands on an unreachable fake domain (webhooks answer 401). Record Payment offers only 1010/1020/1030, so "1025 Payment Gateway Clearing" cannot be used for the fee; payment history is read-only, with no refund or void. My manual journal (Dr 5110 293.32, Dr 1040 1,000, Cr 1020 1,293.32) was accepted against the AR control account with no customer, so AR ageing (32,655) no longer equals 1040 (33,655), contrary to the release notes, and the 1,000 Saadiyat owes is on no ageing or statement. A foreign gateway's fee is a reverse-charge service with no way to record it either.

### Compliance

**F5. Services are booked to "4010 Product Sales" by default (Medium).** This applies to invoices from unbilled time, cost recharges and converted quotes. Only a line-by-line override on manual invoices reaches 4020 Service Revenue. Recurring templates have no revenue account at all. The P&L of a services company reads as product sales.

**F6. Payroll and WPS (Medium).**
- Runs go from calculated to approved, the items show "Paid", and no salary payment is ever posted. 2030 Salaries Payable stayed at 78,000 until I posted the bank journal myself.
- The SIF file is generated, but its SCR header uses the trade licence number ("DIC-98765") as the employer ID. I found no MOHRE establishment ID or employer routing field in Company Profile.
- I would expect the bank to reject this file.

**F7. Dates are shifted by the browser time zone (Medium).**
- A date picked in a Dubai browser is stored as 20:00Z on the previous day. For example, the capital entry dated 1 Jul is numbered JE-20260630-001.
- Ledger reports handle this correctly: the balance sheet at 30 Jun is empty, and VAT and P&L are right.
- The customer statement does not. It prints INV-2026-00001 (dated 6 Jul) as **2026-07-05**, and INV-2026-00007 (dated 20 Aug) as **2026-08-19**. That is a customer-facing document with wrong dates.
- Payments and bills are stored at 00:00Z instead, so storage is inconsistent across document types.

**F8. A manual invoice has no due date and no project (Medium).** The help article says "set the date and due date".

Consequences for replacement invoice INV-7:
- It ages 31–60 days on the statement but 1–30 days on the AR ageing report.
- Payment Chasing says "No overdue invoices. Nice."
- It is unclear whether a late fee could ever attach to it.

**F9. Recurring invoices and late fees cannot be run on demand (Medium).**
- A template backdated to 1 Jul generated nothing in the session. It shows "Next run 1 Jul, Generated 0".
- Late fees are correctly defaulted to "Outside the scope of VAT", but I could not see one raised.

**F10. Gaps in the VAT return header and VAT page (Medium).**
- The return header shows Address "-" and Arabic legal name "-", although the company address is saved. There is no field for an Arabic legal name anywhere.
- The VAT page opens on "Current filing period 1 Oct – 31 Dec" while the Q3 return due on 28 Oct is the one that matters.
- The box figures on the review screen are editable inputs, so a preparer can overwrite engine figures.

### Data integrity and controls

**F11. Status and edit controls on posted documents (Medium).**
- The invoice status is a free dropdown that includes "Paid" and "Credited", so an invoice can be marked paid with no payment.
- Issued invoices still offer "Edit". The help says "an issued invoice is not edited".
- Approved bills offer Edit and Delete.
- Contacts has a red "Clear All" button beside "Download Template".

### Usability, Arabic and mobile

**F12. Usability, Arabic and mobile.**
- Dates: calendar-only pickers (three clicks per month back, English calendars in the Arabic UI); Escape on a calendar can close the whole dialog (I lost a bill payment); payment amount not pre-filled.
- Arabic: the public quote and its acceptance are fully Arabic and right-to-left (good). The invoice PDF has bilingual labels and a clear advance line, but the company name overlaps the TRN in the header, amounts lack thousands separators ("AED 4000.00") and line text stays English. The statement PDF ignores a language parameter. English leaks into the Arabic UI ("Draft", account names, calendars), and switching to Arabic on the public quote page switched my back-office session too.
- 375 px: the Arabic Contacts table collapses (headers truncated, TRN over city) next to a red "Clear All" button; mobile invoice cards have no payment, PDF or credit-note action; at 1024 px the bills table hides its actions column in right-to-left layout. Dashboard, VAT, P&L and project detail were fine.

**F13. Cosmetic.** Timestamp quote number (QT-1790946309966) and a fake number on the manual invoice form until saved; red "Balanced" badge; onboarding pre-fills "Nour Haddad's Company (muqyt5zm)" and drops the signup TRN; no FZ-LLC legal structure; "Annually" offered as a VAT frequency; unlabeled quote action button.

### What worked well

Advance invoices (VAT on receipt, deducted on the final invoice, 2055 back to zero); the reverse-charge posting once the bill exists; VAT, P&L, balance sheet and trial balance tying to the fil to my ledger; emirate attribution of services; partial invoicing from unbilled work; billable supplier costs flowing to "Unbilled costs"; online quote acceptance; gratuity accrual; the 30 Jun cut-off.

## 4. Sign-off

**VAT return: WOULD NOT SIGN.**
- The draft is internally consistent, but I know it is wrong. It declares 3,540 where 3,080 is due, because the product cannot date or size a credit note (F1).
- I would also have had no compliant way to enter the reverse-charge bill without the API (F3).
- To sign, I would need to post the 20 Aug credit as an adjustment outside the product's credit-note flow, and document why.

**Year-end: WOULD NOT SIGN.**
- AR control does not reconcile to the ledger of open invoices (1,000 unallocated, F4).
- The salary payments had to be posted by hand (F6).
- Revenue is misclassified (F5).
- Project profitability is materially wrong (F2).
- The customer statement prints wrong dates (F7).
- Gateway fees and refunds have no supported posting.

## 5. Competitor remark

I would not move this client from Zoho Books, Wafeq or QuickBooks today. Each of those handles the four basics this scenario needs, and Muhasib cannot do any of them in the UI:
1. Partial, dated credit notes with a refund.
2. Reverse charge on a bill with a checkbox.
3. Project-tagged invoices that stay tagged after edits.
4. A working gateway that posts the fee.

Muhasib's VAT engine and advance handling are as good as any of them, and better than QuickBooks for UAE emirate boxes. The ledger arithmetic is trustworthy. The gaps are in workflow completeness, not the maths. Fix F1–F4 and the payroll payment step, and this becomes a credible Wafeq alternative for a services firm.

# Teardown 8, verifier v4: re-verification of the banking and year-end fixes (Desert Rose Trading LLC)

Build 9fba1b61. Fresh database `muhasib_p9_v4b` at http://v4b.localhost:5114, using browser tab tab-23 only. I had no source access. Nearly every step went through the screens: creating my own test API key in Developer settings, plus four read-only API calls (journals, trial balance, P&L / balance sheet and the receivables ageing) and the one lock-test journal. About 40% of the run was in Arabic: locks, lock attacks, unlocking, the asset screens and the reports. I checked six screens at 375 px; none overflowed horizontally.

## Scenario re-run

The run followed my own ledger from Teardown 7:

- **Opening position:** opening balances at 31-12-2023. A journal on 15-03-2024 for the forklift (60,000). FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025 closed.
- **Bank accounts:** an AED account, a USD account and a credit card.
- **Sales:** five AED invoices and one USD 5,000 export invoice. The export was raised on the invoice screen this time.
- **Bills:** four bills, one of them the van (AFM-778, 84,000 + 4,200 VAT).
- **Statement imports:** the same 25 lines in CSV, OFX, MT940 and CAMT.053, plus the USD CSV.
- **Bank rules:** six rules, including a 75/25 salary split and bank charges with and without VAT.
- **Matching:** two invoices settled by one receipt, Falcon's 1,500 overpayment kept as credit, an own-account transfer, the loan drawdown and two split instalments.
- **Fixed assets:** depreciation run to September, disposal on 15-09, and revaluation of the USD account at 3.674.
- **Close:** reconciliation of both accounts, the Q3 VAT draft, a lock on Jul–Sep, lock attacks and a single-month unlock.

## New findings

**N1 (HIGH, money). A posted journal can be reversed twice.**
- After reversing JE-20261001-002 (dated 01-10), the original still shows "Posted" with a working "Reverse" button.
- A second reversal posted JE-20261001-004, and the toast again said "Original entry marked as void".
- The result is a double reversal: 1021 was overstated and 5000 understated by 10.
- I corrected it with JE-20261001-005, dated October, so Q3 is unaffected.

**N2 (MEDIUM, control). The month-end VAT check ignores the quarterly draft.**
- On the September checklist, "VAT return prepared" stays open although the Q3 (Jul–Sep) draft exists.
- Locking September therefore needs the audit-logged override plus a reason. This is a false exception for every quarterly filer.

**N3 (LOW). Preview and naming inconsistencies.**
- The USD receipt match preview shows 5,000 / 5,000 with no FX line, although the posting is correct.
- Unrealised and realised FX both go to 4090.

# Teardown 7, verifier v4: banking, FX, fixed assets and period lock (Desert Rose Trading LLC)

Date: 2 Oct 2026. Server: own database `muhasib_p9_v4`, http://v4.localhost:5104, browser only. Sources: the running app, the help centre, `/api/v1/openapi.json`, and the two permitted docs.

## Scenario summary

Desert Rose Trading LLC is an Abu Dhabi LLC with a 31 December year end and quarterly VAT (TRN 100234567800003). Its setup:

- **Accounts:** ADCB Current AED (1021), ADCB USD (1022) and an ADCB Visa credit card, all created through the screens.
- **Opening position:** opening balances posted at 31-12-2023 (bank 210,000; capital 100,000; retained earnings 110,000). A forklift bought 15-03-2024 for 60,000 was posted by journal. FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025 were closed through Year-end close.

Q3 2026 activity:

- **Sales:** six sales invoices. Five are AED; one is a USD 5,000 zero-rated export at a rate of 3.6725. There is also a VAT invoice for the forklift sale.
- **Bills:** four bills, one of them the van (AFM-778, 84,000 + VAT to 1290).
- **Statement files:** I wrote my own CSV, OFX, MT940 and CAMT.053 files with the same 25 lines, plus a three-line USD CSV.
- **Bank rules:** six rules covering DEWA and Etisalat (5% VAT), salaries split 75/25 between 5020 and 5022, bank charges with and without VAT, and USD SWIFT charges.
- **Receipts:** one receipt settles two invoices; one overpayment of 1,500 is kept as a customer credit; one USD receipt arrives at 3.6735.
- **Transfers and loan:** an AED-to-USD transfer of 36,730 (USD 10,000); a loan drawdown of 200,000 with two split instalments (principal and interest at 9%).
- **Fixed assets:** van FA-0001 (84,000, 4 years, 10% residual) and forklift FA-0002 (bought March 2024, then caught up and disposed of on 15-09 for 40,000 + VAT).
- **Quarter end:** a manual FX revaluation at 3.674, reconciliations of both accounts, the Q3 VAT draft, and locks on July, August and September.
- **Lock tests:** four attempts to post into the locked months.

About a third of the work was done in Arabic: month-end, locking, the lock attacks, reports, reconciliation and fixed assets. Seven screens were checked at 375 px in Arabic. My expected entries were written to a scratch ledger before I looked at the product's output.

## Findings, by severity

### Money / VAT wrong

**F1. A USD receipt books no realised FX, and the USD account can never reconcile.**
- Steps: create a USD invoice at 3.6725. Import a USD statement line of USD 5,000 dated 20-08 (saved rate 3.6735). Accept the suggested "Invoice payment".
- Result: the entry is Dr 1022 / Cr 1040 for AED 18,362.50, at the invoice rate. The USD quantity is then derived at the payment rate (4,998.64).
- Effect: the USD ledger is 1.36 short of the bank, and the reconciliation says "Not balanced" with "Items behind the difference (0)". The realised gain of AED 5.00 is missing from 4090.
- Release notes say "USD accounts reconcile in USD". They do not.
- Verdict: the product is wrong. The receipt must be booked at the receipt-date rate, with the difference to 4090/5140.

**F2. No FX revaluation for foreign bank balances, and the manual workaround corrupts the USD reconciliation.**
- The Exchange Rates screen only remeasures open invoices and bills.
- My month-end journal, Dr 1022 / Cr 4090 for AED 17.50, is treated as a USD movement of 4.76. It appears as a "deposit in transit" that will never clear.
- Effect: there is no correct way, through the screens, to show the USD account at the closing rate and still reconcile it. Of the four reconciliations, the USD one could not be completed.

**F3. The asset register does not tie to the books for a normally bought asset.**
- Steps: post the van bill (AFM-778, coded to 1290). Add FA-0001 in Fixed Assets. Open Fixed Assets → Asset register.
- Result: the van is flagged "not recorded in the books" and left out of the totals (0.00). The register says it differs from 1290−1240 by −79,630.65.
- There is no field to link an asset to its purchase bill. The help article says to enter "the accounts used", but the form has no account fields.
- Effect: the register-vs-ledger control the scenario asks for is unusable for the ordinary bill-then-register path.

**F4. Depreciation is charged after the disposal date and is not reversed on disposal.**
- Steps: run September depreciation (it charges the forklift 1,000 for the full month), then dispose of the forklift dated 15-09.
- Result: the disposal uses accumulated depreciation of 30,548.39 and reports a gain of 10,548.39.
- Help says "depreciation is completed up to the disposal date". In fact 500 of extra depreciation stays in the P&L, overstating depreciation and the gain by 500 each. The disposal is not blocked when depreciation has already been posted past its date.

**F5. Asset disposal has no VAT and no buyer, and the only fix needs three manual steps.**
- The Dispose dialog asks only for a date, an amount and a proceeds account (cash or bank only).
- A sale of a business asset at AED 40,000 is a standard-rated supply of 2,000 VAT for box 1a. That VAT is simply lost unless the user knows to do the following:
  1. Dispose with proceeds of 40,000 into a clearing account.
  2. Raise a sales invoice for 40,000 + VAT to 4080.
  3. Reclassify 40,000 out of 4080 by journal.
  4. Match the 42,000 bank line to the invoice.
- I did this and box 1a is correct. A bookkeeper will not, and will declare 2,000 VAT too little. The bank-line split cannot reach 2020, and rules exclude VAT accounts.

### Compliance / controls

**F6. A locked month cannot be reopened anywhere in the screens.**
- On Month-End Close, a locked period greys out "Lock Period" and "Review Closing Summary". Close History rows have no action.
- The only "Reopen" buttons are for financial years.
- The help article does not describe unlocking a month, and invented routes return 404.
- The scenario step "reopen the quarter as the company owner with a reason" could not be done. Year reopen does work as described: owner only, a reason of at least 10 characters, and "reopen the latest closed year first".

**F7. A historical asset cannot be registered in a closed year, and the error is silent.**
- Steps: Add Asset with a purchase date of 15-03-2024 while 2024 is closed.
- Result: the server answers 403 "Cannot post to locked period (03/2024)", but the dialog shows nothing and stays open.
- Adding an asset posts no journal (the van did not), so the refusal has no reason.
- Workaround: reopen FY2025, then FY2024, add the asset, and close both again. After that the catch-up is correct (see the table). Release notes promise the catch-up "never posts into a closed period". It does not, but you cannot even get to it without reopening two years.

**F8. Month-end checklist and lock.**
- "VAT return prepared: No VAT return prepared for this period" stays red for September although the Q3 draft exists, and the text is untranslated in Arabic.
- Locking is allowed with 2 of 7 checklist items open and the USD account unreconciled, with no warning.

### Usability (things a bookkeeper would get wrong)

**F9. Invoice "Record Payment" cannot deposit into the real bank accounts.**
- Deposit Account lists only 1010, 1020, 1025 and 1030, not 1021 or 1022.
- Bill payment does list them.
- A cheque receipt must go to 1020 and then be moved by journal. Otherwise it never appears as a deposit in transit.

**F10. Invoices have no currency in the UI.**
- The New Invoice and Edit Invoice dialogs have no currency or rate fields.
- The USD invoice could only be created through public API v1 (`currency`, `exchangeRate`). That needed an API key, which is a Professional-plan feature.

**F11. A credit card can only be an asset account.**
- "Add bank account" only allows asset ledger accounts, so the Visa card sits in 1023 under current assets and a balance would show as a negative asset.

**F12. Date pickers have no year or month jump.**
- Posting the 15-03-2024 journal took 31 clicks on "previous month".

**F13. Previews disagree with what is posted.**
- The SWIFT rule preview shows "Dr 5110 15.00" for a USD line that posts AED 55.11.
- The USD receipt preview shows 5,000.00 / 5,000.00.
- The transfer suggestion says "Nothing new is posted" next to a "Post transfer" button, and shows the raw code TRANSFER_BETWEEN_OWN_ACCOUNTS.

**F14. Setup loses data.**
- The TRN typed at sign-up is not saved.
- Running the onboarding "Set up my books now" (the only place to set the emirate; Company Profile has no emirate field) overwrote the TRN already saved in Company Profile with blank.

**F15. Reversing a journal cannot pick a date.**
- The reversal of the 30-09 revaluation was dated today (02-10), and the original still shows "Posted" although the dialog says it is marked void.

**F16. A customer overpayment goes to "2050 Deferred Revenue".**
- It does not appear in the AR ageing, which shows "no rows", so the 1,500 owed to Falcon Contracting cannot be seen anywhere a bookkeeper looks.

## Could everything be done through the screens?

**Yes:**
- bank setup and statement import in four formats, with duplicate detection;
- rules with split lines and VAT;
- multi-invoice receipts and overpayment credit;
- transfers;
- loan split lines;
- the AED reconciliation report;
- depreciation and the closed-year catch-up (once the asset could be added);
- the VAT draft;
- the year close and reopen;
- the month lock.

**No:**
- **USD invoice:** API only.
- **Bank FX revaluation:** manual journal, which then breaks the reconciliation.
- **Reopening a locked month:** impossible.
- **VAT on asset disposal:** three-step workaround.
- **Receipt into the real bank account:** clearing-account workaround.
- **Historical asset in a closed year:** reopen two years first.

## Sign-off

**VAT return Q3 2026: WOULD SIGN.**
- Every box ties to my ledger, including the VAT on rule-posted expenses in box 9.
- The emirate attribution, the zero-rated export and the refund (1,443.75) are right.
- I signed only because I added the disposal VAT myself. A client who used the Dispose screen alone would under-declare box 1a by 2,000.

**Year-end / quarter close: WOULD NOT SIGN.** Reasons:
1. The USD bank cannot be reconciled (F1, F2), and realised and unrealised FX need manual journals that the reconciliation then misreads.
2. The fixed-asset register does not agree with 1290/1240 (F3).
3. Disposal depreciation is misallocated (F4).
4. A locked quarter cannot be reopened for a legitimate correction (F6).

The AED side is genuinely good:
- the reconciliation reaches zero with each reconciling item named;
- duplicates are caught across formats;
- the closed-year catch-up journal is exactly right once reached;
- the lock held against the UI, depreciation runs and the public API.

## Competitor remark

I would not yet move an Abu Dhabi trader with a USD account and fixed assets off Zoho Books or QuickBooks.

- **Both of those** book realised FX on receipt and have a currency revaluation step, so a USD account reconciles in USD.
- **QuickBooks** links an asset to its purchase.
- **Zoho and Wafeq** let you raise a USD invoice on screen.

Muhasib's AED banking (cross-format duplicate detection, rules that write box-9 VAT, a two-sided reconciliation report that explains every item) and its period controls are at least as good as Wafeq's. For an AED-only client without asset sales I would consider the move. Fix F1–F5 and F6 first.
